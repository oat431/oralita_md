# Philosophy

> **Created:** 2026-10-17
> **Part of:** [[00_domain-of-knowledge-overview]] (the crosswalk index) and [[07_kinds-of-knowledge]] (the map theory).
> **The question:** What is philosophy as a domain of knowledge, where do the maps place it, and what does that placement tell us about how it is learned?

## TL;DR

Philosophy studies knowledge, existence, value, and reasoning itself by **conceptual analysis and argument**: it is the meta-domain, whose subject matter is the questions the other domains quietly assume. Aristotle made its highest part the master science ("first philosophy"); the maps then disagree about almost everything else, including whether to list it at all (IB's TOK does not, because the TOK course *is* philosophy). Its product is not doctrine but **better questions**, the deepest kind of progress in [[07_kinds-of-knowledge]]. Since Dewey hosts logic here (160), philosophy is less a neighbor of the other 24 domains than their conscience.

## 1. What the domain studies

Philosophy studies the concepts every other domain uses without examining: knowledge, causation, mind, meaning, justice, existence, truth. Its questions are second-order: not "is this claim true?" but "**what would it even mean** for a claim to be true?"; not "what exists?" but "what does 'exists' mean?"

Progress has its own shape: a sharper distinction (analytic and synthetic), a revealed hidden premise, a question dissolved rather than answered. Great philosophy does not expire: Plato's arguments are still live options. The price is the mirror of mathematics': philosophy rarely settles anything by consensus.

**Logic** rides here as the study of valid inference itself. Dewey files it at 160 inside philosophy; mathematics and computing claim border territory ([[01_mathematics]]).

## 2. Who claims it

| Map | What it calls it / where it sits |
|---|---|
| Aristotle, c. 350 BCE | first philosophy (Aristotle's master science: metaphysics) |
| IB Theory of Knowledge, 2022 | NO AREA: the TOK course itself is philosophy, it studies knowledge rather than listing philosophy as a subject |
| OECD Frascati, 2007 | Humanities 6.3 (philosophy, ethics and religion) |
| Thai Basic Education, 2551 | EMBEDDED (spread across subjects, not a learning area) |
| Dewey Decimal | 100s |
| ISCED-F 2013 | Arts and humanities (02) |
| UNESCO nomenclature, 1973 | Philosophy 72 (its own field) |

Two placements are worth sitting with. The first is Aristotle's: philosophy is not one science among others but "first philosophy," the master science that studies the foundations the rest stand on. It is the only map granting one domain authority over the others: to the one that examines their concepts.

The second is the map's embarrassment: TOK lists five areas of knowledge and philosophy is not among them, because the TOK course is itself philosophy. A map that cannot list its own designer without circularity is confessing that some domains are **frames** rather than contents. The Thai curriculum's "EMBEDDED" is the secular confession.

## 3. Its method and culture

Method family: **argument**. Claims earn trust through conceptual analysis, counterexample, thought experiment, and valid inference: no lab, no archive, no proof from axioms alone (the axioms themselves are up for argument). The unit of progress is a good objection.

In Biglan's terms philosophy is **soft-pure**: low consensus, divergent, aimed at understanding. A good day: you find the hidden premise in a famous argument. Error-correction is slow but real: objections take decades to run their course, and the community's criticism ([[04_logical-fallacies]] in pure form) is aimed at arguments, not data.

## 4. Its seams and neighbors

- [[01_mathematics]]: the foundations seam. Is mathematics logic, empirical science, or free creation? Philosophy questions in mathematics clothes.
- [[21_ethics]]: the value seam. Whether ethics is separable from philosophy is itself a philosophical question: Aristotle filed ethics under practical philosophy.
- [[22_religion-and-theology]]: the metaphysics seam: cosmological arguments, the problem of evil, the nature of faith: the overlap is almost total.
- [[10_psychology]]: the mind seam: philosophy of mind and cognitive science, same objects, different depths.

## 5. How to learn this domain

> Status: STUB. The full treatment comes in the meta-learning note on learning each kind of knowledge (question 2).

The argument family's practice is **reading and writing arguments**, and the practitioner's deed is arguing: reconstruct a position so well its defender would accept it, then find the load-bearing premise. Philosophy is learned by writing, not reading: the skill is rebuilding an argument and testing it against the best objections. The tools of [[04_logical-fallacies]] and [[06_disagreeing-without-rage]] are the domain's own instruments.

## Key takeaways

- Philosophy is the meta-domain: it studies the concepts the other domains use without examining.
- Its method is argument: analysis, counterexample, and inference decide what counts, slowly but permanently.
- The maps' most revealing move is refusing to list it: TOK is philosophy, Thai education embeds it everywhere, Aristotle crowns it master science.
- Its product is better questions, not final answers: progress of the kind [[07_kinds-of-knowledge]] describes.
- To learn it is to argue it: rebuild the arguments yourself; reading philosophy without writing it is tourism.
