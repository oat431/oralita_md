# Business and Management

> **Created:** 2026-10-17
> **Part of:** [[00_domain-of-knowledge-overview]] (the crosswalk index) and [[07_kinds-of-knowledge]] (the map theory).
> **The question:** What is business and management as a domain of knowledge, where do the maps place it, and what does that placement tell us about how it is learned?

## TL;DR

Business and management studies organizations, markets, and the practical work of getting collective action done: **judgment under uncertainty** as a knowledge discipline. Its ancestor in Aristotle is oikonomia, household management; management as we know it is among the newest professional knowledges. Its signature practice is the **case method** (MBA-style): learn judgment by deciding real cases and defending them. Two maps refuse it a field of its own (Thai education files it under การงานอาชีพ; UNESCO has none): knowledge mid-way from trade to discipline.

## 1. What the domain studies

Business and management studies how people organize to produce, decide, and endure: strategy, operations, organizations, markets, finance, coordination. Its questions are practical and urgent: "what should this organization do now, with these resources, in this uncertainty?", "why did this strategy work here and fail there?"

Progress is knowledge that survives consequences: a framework that clarifies a messy decision, evidence about what raises performance, a case explaining why a firm fell. The results are not laws but **judgment**: knowing which framework fits which situation, what to do when frameworks disagree. This is management as phronesis plus social science: Aristotle's practical wisdom in modern clothes.

The domain is honest about its uncertainty: decisions are made with incomplete information and checked far later, so the discipline prizes calibration and treats arrogance as an operational risk: [[05_dunning-kruger-effect]] in its most expensive form.

## 2. Who claims it

| Map | What it calls it / where it sits |
|---|---|
| Aristotle, c. 350 BCE | practical (oikonomia: household management) |
| IB Theory of Knowledge, 2022 | Human sciences |
| OECD Frascati, 2007 | Social sciences 5.2 (economics and business) |
| Thai Basic Education, 2551 | การงานอาชีพ (Career and Technology) |
| Dewey Decimal | 650 (management) |
| ISCED-F 2013 | Business, administration and law (04) |
| UNESCO nomenclature, 1973 | NO FIELD! Nearest: Economic Sciences 53 |

The table's most interesting cell is UNESCO's: **no field at all**. An entire domain of modern professional life, absent from the 1973 nomenclature: Dewey gives management 650, ISCED its own broad field (04). The domain is young: management as a taught discipline with its own methods and degrees consolidated in the twentieth century, after these categories were imagined. UNESCO 1973 did not reject it; it had not yet seen it.

The Thai curriculum's answer points the same way: การงานอาชีพ (Career and Technology), a learning area about careers and making-do, not a science of organizations. The map with no room for management as knowledge trains students in it as practice: the knowledge-versus-profession seam again.

## 3. Its method and culture

Method family: **normative and social**: social science for the diagnosis (economics, psychology, organizations), normative judgment for the decision (what should we do?). The signature practice is the **case method**: a real organization, a real decision point, a student who must commit and defend the answer against classmates arguing the opposite. Frameworks travel; unjudged ones do damage.

In Biglan's terms management is **soft-applied**: divergent, application-oriented, converging only on well-studied questions. A good day: a messy situation becomes legible; a decision is made with reasons written down; a month later the result shows which held. Error-correction is slow (organizations are noisy laboratories) and socially costly, so cases and history carry much of its knowledge: vicarious experience is judgment's cheap rehearsal.

## 4. Its seams and neighbors

- [[11_economics]]: the theory-versus-practice seam. Economics models the market; management acts inside one firm's messy reality: they share vocabulary and disagree about how much it helps.
- [[23_law]]: the compliance seam: every decision runs through contracts, regulation, liability; the border is negotiated daily.
- [[24_education]]: the training seam: management is taught by case, apprenticeship, simulation: its pedagogy is part of its knowledge.

## 5. How to learn this domain

> Status: STUB. The full treatment comes in the meta-learning note on learning each kind of knowledge (question 2).

The practice here is the **case method**, and the practitioner's deed is deciding: commit to a course of action with reasons, and defend it while competent people attack it. Management is learned by deciding and reviewing decisions: the skill is judgment under uncertainty, which only exists in the deed. Cases, simulations, and post-mortems are its problem sets; calibration is the quiet core.

## Key takeaways

- Management is judgment under uncertainty: phronesis plus social science, among the newest professional knowledges.
- UNESCO 1973 has no field for it at all: the domain's youth shows on the map; Dewey (650) and ISCED (04) already house it.
- Its practice is the MBA case method: decide, defend, review: judgment is trained by the deed.
- The seam with economics is theory versus practice; the seam with law is compliance; both are negotiated daily.
- To learn it is to decide it: cases, simulations, post-mortems, with calibration keeping arrogance out.
