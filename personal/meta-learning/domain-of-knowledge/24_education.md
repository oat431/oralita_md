# Education

> **Created:** 2026-10-17
> **Part of:** [[00_domain-of-knowledge-overview]] (the crosswalk index) and [[07_kinds-of-knowledge]] (the map theory).
> **The question:** What is education as a domain of knowledge, where do the maps place it, and what does that placement tell us about how it is learned?

## TL;DR

Education studies teaching and learning themselves: the **second meta-domain** of this folder, after philosophy, with transmitting knowledge as its subject matter. Its method is classroom studies, design research, and evidence on what works: soft-applied with a normative wing. The telling row is its own: Thai basic education gives education **no home**, because education is not a learning area but the system containing the learning areas: the map's designer cannot appear as an entry on the map. That paradox says more about the maps than about the domain.

## 1. What the domain studies

Education studies how people learn and how that learning is deliberately supported: teaching, curriculum, assessment, development, institutions, and the unequal distribution of all four. Its questions: "what helps this learner learn this thing?", "how do we know?", "what is worth teaching, and who decides?"

Progress is practical and cumulative: a teaching method that reliably beats the alternative, an assessment that measures what it claims to, a curriculum sequence that respects how understanding grows, a study that explains why a reform failed. The domain sits between theory and practice in a way neither pole fully owns: a learning-science finding is not yet a teaching technique, and a teacher's craft knowledge is not yet a theory.

The subject matter includes the reader directly: everything in this folder claims knowledge; education asks how any of it gets from one head to another. The teaching-knowledge thread of [[03_knowledge-communication]] is this domain's export to everyone else.

## 2. Who claims it

| Map | What it calls it / where it sits |
|---|---|
| Aristotle, c. 350 BCE | practical (education for virtue) |
| IB Theory of Knowledge, 2022 | Human sciences |
| OECD Frascati, 2007 | Social sciences 5.3 (educational sciences) |
| Thai Basic Education, 2551 | NO HOME: education is not a learning area, it is the system that contains the learning areas |
| Dewey Decimal | 370 |
| ISCED-F 2013 | Education (01) |
| UNESCO nomenclature, 1973 | Pedagogy 58 (its own field) |

The most interesting placement is the Thai curriculum's: a paradox worth keeping. A curriculum is a map of what students should learn, and education builds curricula: so "education" cannot appear as a learning area inside a document education itself produced. The map's designer is invisible on the map. UNESCO's nomenclature avoids the paradox by fiat: Pedagogy 58 is simply its own field.

The maps also split between two accounts of what education is: ISCED's **Education (01)** is a professional field, training teachers; the OECD's **5.3 educational sciences** is a social science, researching learning. Both are real: the domain is at once a science of learning and a profession of teaching, the knowledge-versus-practice seam again.

## 3. Its method and culture

Method family: **soft-applied with a normative core**. Claims earn trust through classroom studies, design research (build it, test it in real classrooms, iterate), and evidence syntheses of what works. But the domain never escapes the normative: "what works" always works *toward* something, and choosing the aim (virtue for Aristotle, employability, flourishing) is a values question evidence cannot settle.

In Biglan's terms education is **soft-applied**: divergent in theory, convergent on practice. A good day: a lesson that failed last term works this term and you know why; a study explains a result every good teacher already knew, and adds when it breaks. Error-correction is slow and noisy (children, schools, and years resist clean experiments), so the domain respects evidence and experienced judgment.

## 4. Its seams and neighbors

- [[10_psychology]]: the learning seam. Cognitive and developmental psychology supply theories of the learner; education supplies the real-world test.
- [[21_ethics]]: the aims seam. What is education *for*, and who decides? Every pedagogy smuggles in an ethics of the good life.
- [[20_philosophy]]: the foundations seam: philosophy of mind (what is understanding?) and epistemology (what counts as knowing?) overlap with curriculum theory.

## 5. How to learn this domain

> Status: STUB. The full treatment comes in the meta-learning note on learning each kind of knowledge (question 2).

The practice here is **teaching and design research**, and the practitioner's deed is teaching: prepare, teach, watch what happens to understanding, and revise. Education is learned by teaching, because the central skill is what only the deed trains: reading a room of minds and adjusting. The domain is therefore reflexive: its practitioners study the very process they use to learn it.

## Key takeaways

- Education is the second meta-domain: the study of teaching and learning, the machinery by which the other domains are transmitted.
- It is both a science of learning (OECD 5.3) and a profession of teaching (ISCED Education 01): the maps split along the seam.
- The Thai curriculum gives it no home: education is the system containing the learning areas, and the map's designer cannot be an entry.
- Its method mixes evidence (classroom studies, design research) with irreducibly normative questions about aims.
- To learn it is to teach it: the deed of teaching is the only place the central skill lives.
