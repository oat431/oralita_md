# Meta-Learning: How to Learn and Argue Well

> **Created:** 2026-10-13
> **What this is:** the front door of the meta-learning series. It started with one question ("How do I read a research paper without fooling myself?") and grew into how knowledge works, how it moves between people, how reasoning fails, how to disagree well, and how knowledge is carved into kinds. Seven notes, one web.
> **Reading order:** 01 to 07, top to bottom. The wikilinks connect everything, so any entry point works.

## How this series works

- Every note has the same shape: **The question** at the top, a TL;DR, numbered sections, Knowledge connections, and Key takeaways.
- All notes carry the tag **#meta-learning**: search the tag to find them from anywhere in the vault.
- Files are numbered in reading order; new notes append as 07, 08, ... when a real question appears (not on a schedule).
