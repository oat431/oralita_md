## Format & Structure Fixes

### ❌ Current Problems
1. Two-column layout breaks ATS parsing
2. Non-standard section ordering
3. Inconsistent date formatting
4. Contact info appears mid-document (parsing error)

### ✅ Recommended Structure
```
1. Name + Headline (e.g., "Software Engineer (Full Stack)")
2. Contact Information (single line, icons optional)
3. Professional Summary (3-4 lines max)
4. Technical Skills (categorized)
5. Work Experience (reverse chronological)
6. Education
7. Projects (optional)
8. Certifications (optional)
```

### ATS-Friendly Formatting Rules
- ✅ Use single-column layout
- ✅ Standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Garamond) at 10-12pt
- ✅ Standard section headings ("Work Experience", not "My Journey")
- ✅ Save as PDF with professional filename: `Sahachan_Tippimwong_Software_Engineer_CV.pdf`
- ❌ No tables, graphics, or unusual fonts
- ❌ No headers/footers (ATS often can't read them)

2. **Location/relocation** — Indicate if you're willing to move to Bangkok
3. **Salary expectations** — Some Thai companies expect this on the resume (optional)

5. **Stability** — Show commitment (your 2+ years at Gosoft is good)
