# Run/Walk Log — Template

> The marathon (12–18 month goal) runs on this file. One row per **structured run/walk session**. Fill immediately after the run while the watch still has the workout on screen.

## Frequency (important)

- **Structured sessions: 1×/week (Wednesday) for now.** At 121 kg, daily run-walk is joint risk — dose control comes first. This scales to 2, then 3–4 sessions/week months from now, at lower bodyweight, only after this log has real data.
- **Daily walking is separate** — it goes in the steps column of `daily-check-in.md`, not here.
- Morning walk before work = encouraged. Walking, not jogging.

## How to get the numbers (Xiaomi Watch S4)

1. Start a **Running (outdoor)** workout before the first jog interval.
2. After the session: workout history → this run → read **duration, distance, avg pace, avg HR**.
3. If the watch shows time-in-zone, grab Zone 2 as well.

## Targets

| Metric | Target | Why |
| ------ | ------ | --- |
| Minutes | 30 min exactly (no bonus running) | Dose control at 121 kg |
| Avg HR | **117–137 bpm** (Zone 2) | Fat oxidation peak + aerobic base (see `[[smart-watch-settings]]`) |
| Protocol | 4 min jog / 2 min walk × 5 | Phase 3 adjusts only from logged data |

**Above 137 avg → slow the jogs.** Below 117 → you're walking; fine, that's the honest data.

## Why minutes alone failed in Phase 2

W5–W8: minutes logged 0 of 4 weeks. Zero run-progression decisions were possible. This log makes pace and HR the deliverable, not the afterthought.
