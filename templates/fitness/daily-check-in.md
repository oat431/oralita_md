**Columns explained**

- **Sleep** — hours, from watch or estimate.
- **Weight** — morning, fasted. Don't react to single-day spikes (water/sodium/glycogen). Judge the 7-day average.
- **Protein** — did you hit 160 g+? ✓ or ✗. 7×✓ per week is the standard.
- **Kcal** — structure score from `food-log.md`: ✓ = day assembled within structure (~2,000–2,200), ▲ = over (fried/dessert/liquid calories), ⚠ = under (missed protein anchor). 6/7 ✓ per week = target.
- **Steps** — from watch.
- **Notes** — pain, form issues, missed meal, illness, anything the Sunday review needs to know.
