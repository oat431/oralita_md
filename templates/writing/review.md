# Review Templates

> Fill-in masters per medium. Copy ONE block into `writing/review/`. Method, criteria discipline & failure modes: [[01-Review-Writing-Guide]]. Name live files `YYYYMMDD-<title>.md`.
>
> **Language-neutral by design:** criteria → evidence → verdict works in any language; draft in your audience's language.

## 🎬 Movie

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [review, movie]
rating: 
---

# <Title> (<Year>) — Review

**Watched:** theater/streaming · **Runtime:** <Xh mm> · **Spoilers:** none | marked below

## Hook
<your shardest one-line observation>

## What it's trying to be
<genre + ambition, 1–2 sentences>

## Evaluation
- **Story:** <does it hold together? evidence>
- **Acting / Direction:** <standouts, misfires>
- **Visual & Sound:** <cinematography, score — only if they affect you>
- **Pacing:** <where did time fly / drag?>

## Weaknesses
- <specific flaw + why it matters>

## Verdict
**Score:** X/10 · **In one phrase:** "<matinee/full-price style call>"
**Watch it if…** <profile> · **Skip if…** <profile>
```
