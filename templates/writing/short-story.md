# Short Story Templates

> Fill-in masters for fiction practice. Copy ONE block into `writing/short_story/`. Structures & scene tools explained: [[02-Storytelling-Guide]]. Name live files `YYYYMMDD-<slug>.md`.
>
> **Language-neutral by design:** beats and checklists transfer to any language — draft in the language your story thinks in.

## Standard Short Story

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [fiction, short-story]
status: draft
words: 0
---

# <Working Title>

**Logline:** When <inciting incident>, <protagonist — with one defining trait> must <goal>, or else <stakes>.
**POV / Tense:** first/third · past/present · **Target:** ~2,000 words
**Structure:** Story Circle | Three-act | Kishōtenketsu

## Arc beats
1. Comfort zone — who they are before:
2. Need (what they chase):
3. Go (world changes):
4. Search (adapt, struggle):
5. Find (they get it — almost):
6. Take (the price paid): ⚠️ this is where the story earns its ending
7. Return:
8. Change (proof, not statement):

## Scene list
| # | Goal → Conflict → Disaster/Turn | Beat served |
|---|--------------------------------|-------------|
| 1 |  |  |

## Craft checklist before drafting ends
- [ ] Opens late (in the middle of something)
- [ ] Every scene changes something
- [ ] Ending echoes the opening image
- [ ] Cut any line that only delivers information
```

## Flash Fiction (~300 words)

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [fiction, flash]
words: 0
---

# <Title>

**The one turn:** <what single reversal carries everything?>

1. Want in one image:
2. Obstacle in one action:
3. THE TURN:
4. Echo ending (reimage step 1):

Rule: cut every character except the essential one.
```

> ⚠️ Reminder from the knowledge layer: kishōtenketsu works beautifully for quiet pieces — Ki (set the scene) → Sho (deepen) → Ten (new angle / turn) → Ketsu (connect back) — no conflict engine required.
