# Persuasive Templates

> Fill-in masters per form. Copy ONE block into place. Method & failure modes: [[07-Persuasive-Writing-Guide]]. Name live files `YYYYMMDD-<slug>.md`.
>
> **Language-neutral by design:** claim → evidence → counterargument carries in any language; write in your audience's language.

## 📝 Opinion / Argumentative Essay

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [persuasive, essay]
---

# <Title — can state the thesis directly>

**Thesis:** <one arguable sentence — someone could reasonably disagree>

## Why this matters now
<stakes for the reader>

## Evidence
### <Point 1>
<claim → evidence → reasoning back to thesis>
### <Point 2>
<claim → evidence → reasoning back to thesis>

## Counterargument
<the strongest opposing view, stated fairly — steelman it>
<your rebuttal>

## Conclusion
<what the reader should now believe or do>
```

## 📣 Copywriting / Ad (AIDA)

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [persuasive, copy]
---

# <Product/Campaign>

## Attention (headline/hook)
<the line that stops the scroll>

## Interest (why this is relevant to THEM)
<their problem, named>

## Desire (benefit, not feature)
<feature: what it has → benefit: what it does for them>

## Action
<ONE clear CTA — never two>
```

**Filled example**

```markdown
## Attention
Your backups are only as good as the restore you never tested.

## Interest
Most homelab backups fail silently — you find out at the worst time.

## Desire
Feature: automated monthly restore drills.
Benefit: you sleep knowing the restore actually works, not just the backup.

## Action
Set up your first drill this weekend → [one link]
```

## 🎯 Proposal / Pitch

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [persuasive, proposal]
---

# Proposal: <Title>

## Problem
<the pain, specific, with cost of inaction>

## Solution
<what you propose, concretely>

## Why us / why this approach
<credibility — track record, unique angle>

## The ask
<exactly what you want approved — budget, time, decision — ONE thing>
```

## Quick Check (before sending)

- [ ] Thesis is arguable — someone could reasonably disagree with it
- [ ] Strongest counterargument addressed, not strawmanned
- [ ] One CTA, not three
- [ ] Every feature translated to a benefit
- [ ] The ask is explicit, not implied
