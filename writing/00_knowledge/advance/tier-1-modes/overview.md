# Rhetorical Modes — Overview

> **Tier 1** of the advanced writing reference. A **rhetorical mode** answers one question: *how does this text develop its ideas?* Eight modes exist; a real piece of writing runs on one primary mode plus one or two supporting modes.

## The Eight Modes

| # | Mode | What it does | Fundamental guide |
|---|------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1 | [[Descriptive-Mode]] | Make the reader see, hear, or feel the subject | — |
| 2 | [[Narrative-Mode]] | Present events as a meaningful sequence | [[02-Storytelling-Guide]] |
| 3 | [[Expository-Mode]] | Inform, define, clarify, or explain | [[06-Expository-Writing-Guide]] |
| 4 | [[Analytical-Mode]] | Separate a subject into parts and interpret their relationships | — |

| 8 | [[Reflective-Mode]] | Turn experience into transferable learning | — |

## Modes Blend

Real pieces are **hybrids**. A movie review is expository (plot facts) plus descriptive (the look of it) plus persuasive (the verdict). A campaign log is narrative carried by description. Naming which mode each paragraph is doing lets you cut paragraphs doing no job.

One mode is always **dominant**; supporting modes must serve it, not compete with it.
