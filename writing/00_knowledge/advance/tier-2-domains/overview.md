# Writing Domains — Overview

> **Tier 2** of the advanced writing reference. A **domain** is a social or professional context with its own objectives, conventions, evidence standards, and vocabulary. Where a mode describes *how* a text thinks, a domain describes *where* it lives and *whose* rules it follows.

## Mode × Domain

A domain is not a mode. **Argumentation** is a *mode* that appears in academic articles, business proposals, and opinion columns alike. An **academic article** is a *genre* within the *academic domain*. Keep the three axes separate and you avoid the most common classification error.
