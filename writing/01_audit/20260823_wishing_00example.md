# Audit — `wishing/00_example` (birthday wish, Mr.คมชาญ)

> Audited 2026-08-23 against [[04-Wishing-Note-Guide]]. Contract: problems first · cited evidence · `[objective]` vs `[taste]`.

## Summary Judgment

A coherent philosophy essay wearing a birthday wish's clothes. The existential theme (we create our own meaning) is consistent and sincerely meant — but the recipient is anonymous inside it. Run the guide's test — *"could this be sent to any other person unchanged?"* — and the answer is yes. That is the one failure this genre cannot survive.

## Problems (cited)

1. **[objective] No ground — recipient invisible.** Beyond "Mr.คมชาญ", nothing in the text references him: no shared moment, trait, or achievement. Per the guide, specificity is the *proof* the writer thought of them; without it, this reads as forwardable template text — which collides directly with failure mode #1 (copy-paste energy).
2. **[objective] Abstract stacking, textbook case.** "ขอให้คุณได้รับความสุข ความสงบ และความรู้สึกที่สดใส" + "ความกล้าในการเลือกทางที่คุณรัก และค้นหาความหมายในแต่ละวัน" — five abstract nouns, zero concrete hopes tied to *his* actual goals. This is failure mode #4 verbatim.
3. **[objective] Redundancy loops.** "ในขณะที่คุณทำความเข้าใจและเข้าใจชีวิต" — "เข้าใจ" doubled back-to-back (editing slip). "ความหมาย" appears ~5 times; "สำรวจ/ค้นพบ/การสำรวจและการค้นพบ" recycle in the closing stretch. The theme circles instead of advancing.
4. **[objective] Register splits down the middle.** Opens with 🎉 (casual, social), closes with "ขอแสดงความนับถือ" (business-letter formal). Two different relationships speak in one note. Pick the person, then pick the register.
5. **[taste] The philosophical frame does the emotional work the *detail* should do.** "ชีวิตของเรามีความหมายเพราะเราเองสร้างความหมายให้มัน" is a sentiment anyone could receive. The kindest version of this wish would keep ONE philosophical line and spend the rest on something only you two share.

## What Works

- **Thematic unity** — the meaning-making idea opens, develops, and closes the piece. Most amateur wishes have no idea at all; this has exactly one.
- Clean spelling/grammar throughout.
- The single best sentence is the most personal one: "ขอให้คุณมีความกล้าในการเลือกทางที่คุณรัก" — courage-to-choose points at a real human decision. It's the seed the whole note should grow from.

## Fix Priority (next draft)

1. Add one GROUND line: a thing he did, survived, built, or said this year
2. Convert two of the abstract nouns into one concrete hope tied to that ground
3. Resolve the register: 🎉-register gets "สุขสันต์วันเกิดนะครับ"-style warmth at the close too; formal register loses the emoji
4. Cut to ≤ half current length by deleting every repetition of ความหมาย except one
