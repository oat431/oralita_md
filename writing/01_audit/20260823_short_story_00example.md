# Audit — `short_story/00_example` (ฝนตกที่เชียงใหม่)

> Audited 2026-08-23 against [[02-Storytelling-Guide]]. Contract: problems first · cited evidence · `[objective]` vs `[taste]`.

## Summary Judgment

The strongest of the three examples — and quietly, the most structurally sophisticated. First, a genre note `[objective, minor]`: this is filed under short_story but it is **not fiction** — it's a personal reflective essay (true events shaped like a story). That's the memoir lane of the narrative family, and it's a perfectly good home for it; just label it honestly. Second, the headline: this piece is a **native kishōtenketsu** — you built a contrast-led structure without any conflict engine, exactly the East Asian form we discussed as your birthright.

## The Kishōtenketsu Map (proof it works)

| Part | Lines | Content |
|------|-------|---------|
| Ki — introduce | 1–3 | Rain in cold season, "like every year," now in Chiang Mai; his drift toward loneliness |
| Sho — deepen | 3 | The pattern: rain = thinking = finding loneliness inside |
| **Ten — turn** | 5 | "แต่ฝนตกในปีนี้กลับไม่เหมือนทุกปีเพราะผมดันติดฝนกับเพื่อนๆ" — the contrast flips the frame |
| Ketsu — connect | 7–9 | Rain re-read: it gathered people; gratitude; realization of growth |

No antagonist, no battle — pure development-and-recontextualization. Textbook, and I mean that as high praise.

## Problems (cited)

1. **[objective] The final sentence tangles the piece's core emotion.** "ผ่านเรื่องราวแก้เหงาให้เพื่อนๆฟัง😶" — who was cured of loneliness? Lines 3–5 established *his* loneliness; suddenly the stories cure *the friends*. If both were lonely and the rain cured everyone, that's the best idea in the piece — but the grammar buries it mid-clause. The last line of an essay carries the change; this one stumbles on arrival.
2. **[objective] Mechanics slips.** Line 7: "และและผมใช้เวลา" — doubled conjunction (edit slip). Line 5: "แต่อะไรก็ช่างการนั่งเล่า..." — clause crashes into the next with no punctuation, then "ช่าง...ช่าง" repeats within the same sentence. Line 7 again: "หลายๆคนหลายๆกลุ่มที่อยู่สถานที่เดียวกับผม" — "อยู่สถานที่" reads clunky.
3. **[objective, minor] Told-not-shown ending.** The change arrives as announcement: "ผมกล้ามากขึ้นในหลายๆเรื่อง" (I became braver in many things). Bravery is abstract; the piece *showed* growth already — staying, talking, being present. Ending on the concrete image instead of the abstract label would let the reader conclude "braver" themselves.
4. **[taste] Hedge-stacking softens the strong parts.** "(แต่มันก็ขึ้นอยู่ดี)", "หรือบางทีผมอาจจะคิดมากไป", "ไม่รู้เลย..." — the self-deprecating shrug is charming once; three times it becomes a tic that undercuts your own observations. The 😌 line about other groups is your best detail precisely because it doesn't hedge.
5. **[taste] Emoji as beat-markers.** Four different emojis in nine lines (😊😆😌😶) do tonal work the words should own — and 😶 on the final line visually mutes the ending you want ringing.
6. **[objective, minor] Apology opening.** "ผมไม่ได้นั่งเขียนอะไรยาวๆมานานแล้ว" is throat-clearing — enter late, leave early applies to openings too.

## What Works

- **The Ten is genuinely earned** — the turn isn't manufactured; it's a real memory that genuinely contradicts his rain=loneliness pattern.
- **Sensory anchor:** "เสียงประกอบที่เป็นเม็ดฝนกระทบหลังคา" — rain-as-soundtrack gives the scene its audio; that's show-don't-tell working.
- **The outward glance** (other groups around them doing the same 😌) universalizes a private moment — the move that separates essays from diary entries.
- The `.` stanza separators create breathing rhythm — effective, keep them.

2. Fix "และและ", punctuate the line-5 pile-up
3. Delete one hedge (keep the best one), delete one emoji
4. Cut the apology opening; start at "ฝนตกตอนฤดูหนาว..."
