# Home Gym Shopping List

> Phase 3 starts with what's in the house (treadmill, 10 kg KB, 2 kg DBs, push-up/sit-up machines, mat). These purchases fill the loading gaps. Prices are Thai retail ranges — Lazada / Shopee / Decathlon / Mr. DIY.

## Tier 1 — buy now (~500–2,000 ฿ total)

| Item | Why | ~Price | Look for |
|------|-----|--------|----------|
| **Spinlock dumbbell handles + plates** (2 handles, plates: 4×5 kg + 4×2.5 kg + 4×1.25 kg) | The single most important purchase. Unlocks DB floor press, OHP, rows, RDL, curls at real loads. Pairs build from ~2 kg to ~18 kg per hand | 1,000–1,600 ฿ | 25–28 mm bar diameter, cast-iron plates, spinlock collars with rubber grip |
| **Resistance band set** (tube band + door anchor, or loop set: light/medium/heavy) | Face pulls, band rows, pallof press, tricep work, glute activation | 200–400 ฿ | Tube with door anchor = most versatile. Avoid the thinnest "starter" sets that snap |
| **Doorway pull-up bar** | Dead hangs for grip + lat activation now; negatives later; full pull-ups are a Phase 4+ goal at 120 kg | 300–600 ฿ | CHECK YOUR DOOR FRAME FIRST — measure the width and whether the trim allows a lever-mount bar |

**Buy plates, not fixed dumbbells.** Fixed pairs cost more and lock you out of micro-progression (1.25 kg jumps matter at home).

## Tier 2 — later, if Phase 3 goes well (2,000–6,000 ฿)

| Item | Why | When |
|------|-----|------|
| **Adjustable bench** | Turns floor press into full-ROM DB press; incline pressing | When floor press stalls at top range AND space allows |
| **16 kg kettlebell** | The 10 kg bell becomes warm-up weight; swings/goblet need the step up | When KB swing 3×15 @ 10 kg is easy with clean form |

## Tier 3 — only if the day-pass gym option dies

| Item | Why | When |
|------|-----|------|
| Barbell + plates + rack (or squat stands) | Real loading for squat/bench/DL at home | Only if you stop being able to visit a day-pass gym at all. ~10,000+ ฿ and needs floor space + flooring |

## What NOT to buy

- **Fixed dumbbell sets** — expensive, no micro-loading.
- **Spring-grip bars** — dangerous at real weights, stick to spinlock.
- **More ab machines** — you have a sit-up machine; planks/dead bugs are free and better.
- **Cheap "ab belts"/EMS gadgets** — toys, not training.
- **Another kettlebell before the 10 kg is genuinely easy** — it isn't yet.

*Rule: every purchase must solve a specific loading gap in the program. If you're unsure, ask before buying — a 500 ฿ band set you use 3×/week beats a 15,000 ฿ machine that becomes a clothes rack.*
