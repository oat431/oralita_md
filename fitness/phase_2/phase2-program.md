# Phase 2 — Barbell Foundation (Weeks 5–8)

> **Live plan:** Week 7 targets are shown below. Historical review data is stored in `weekly-review-summary.md`.

## Purpose

Build barbell technique and steady strength progression while losing fat, preserving muscle, and building a low-impact aerobic base.

**Training days:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Saturday  
**Rest days:** Thursday, Friday, Sunday

## Schedule

| Day | Focus | Key work | Time |
|-----|-------|----------|------|

| Tue | Lower | Back squat + lower accessories | ~55 min |
| Wed | Run/walk + core | 30-minute intervals | ~40 min |
| Sat | Full body | Deadlift + front squat | ~60 min |

## Progression Rules

1. Complete every prescribed rep with good form → add **2.5 kg** to the next barbell session.
2. Barbell reps are missed or form is borderline → repeat the same load.
3. The same lift misses twice in a row → reduce the load by **10%** and rebuild.
4. Accessories marked **Hard** → repeat the load. Accessories marked **Easy** with clean form → add one small increment.
5. **Three working sets means three.** Do not add bonus sets, exercises, or running.
6. Sharp pain, joint pain, back pain, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath → stop and report it. Muscle burn is not the same as sharp pain.

### Logging Convention

- Log working sets only; warm-up sets do not count.
- Use one working load across all prescribed sets unless the program explicitly says otherwise.
- Dumbbell weight means **per hand**.
- Machine weight means the displayed stack/load value; do not assume it equals free-weight kilograms.
- Record actual reps and any form limitation immediately after the session.

## Session Protocol

1. **General warm-up:** 5–8 minutes of cycling or treadmill walking plus dynamic movement.
2. **Barbell warm-up sets:** before each barbell lift:
   - approximately 50% × 5
   - approximately 70% × 3
   - approximately 90% × 2
   - then the prescribed working sets
3. Warm-ups are for preparation and technique; do not log them as working sets.
4. Rest 2–3 minutes for barbell compounds, 90 seconds for machine compounds, and 60 seconds for isolations.
5. Stop adding work when the listed session is complete.

## Nutrition and Recovery Targets

- **Calories:** 2,200 kcal/day. Do not change calories because of one scale reading.
- **Protein:** minimum 160 g/day.
- **Water:** 3–4 L/day.
- **Sleep:** 7 hours minimum.
- **Weight:** morning weigh-in after bathroom, before food or water.
- **Smart scale:** standardized reading every Sunday morning.
- **Wearable:** bring weekly average steps, PAI, sleep, and a valid resting-HR metric to the Sunday review.

Active-balance adjustments:

- 7-day average falls >0.6 kg/week for 2 consecutive weeks → add 200 kcal/day.
- 7-day average falls <0.15 kg/week for 3 weeks → audit adherence or reduce 150 kcal/day.
- Weekly sleep average <6 hours → remove one quality session.

## Optional Morning Movement — 10 kg Kettlebell

This is supplementary movement, **not a fifth workout**, and it cannot selectively burn stomach fat. Fat loss comes from the overall calorie deficit; the kettlebell develops basic hinge, squat, carry, and trunk capacity.

- **Daily priority:** 10-minute brisk walk to help reach the 5,000-step weekly average.
- **Maximum kettlebell frequency:** 2 mornings/week, 8–10 minutes, RPE 5/10 or easier.
- **Thursday:** 2 rounds — kettlebell deadlift × 8, goblet squat × 8, suitcase carry 20–30 sec/side, dead bug × 6/side. Rest 60–90 sec.
- **Sunday:** repeat only if Saturday soreness is minimal; otherwise walk only.
- No kettlebell session on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, or Saturday.
- Do not add swings, cleans, snatches, or Turkish get-ups until the hinge is consistently pain-free and technically controlled.

The kettlebell must not reduce performance in the four scheduled sessions.
