# Tuesday — Lower

Focus: Back squat + leg accessories. Time: ~55 min.

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (cycle/walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]`

---

## 1. Barbell Back Squat

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/1436-Gnfo4FM.gif)

- **Warm-up ladder:** (rest 60–90 sec between ramp sets)
	- 20 kg (bar) × 5
	- 30 kg × 3
	- 40 kg × 2 
- **Working:** 
	- 3 × 5 @ **42.5 kg** — rest 2–3 min
- **Standard:** ONE load across all 3 sets — no mid-session drops (W7's 45→40→40 was invalid). Brace hard; thighs parallel or below.

## 2. Leg Press

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0739-10Z2DXU.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 
	- 1 light set — 30–35 kg × 10–12 (first leg machine; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 
	- 3 × 8 @ **62.5 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** controlled depth; do not lock the knees

## 3. Lying Leg Curl

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0586-17lJ1kr.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 
	- none — hamstrings warm from squat + leg press
- **Working:** 
	- 3 × 10 @ **30 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** two-second lowering phase

## 4. Calf Raises

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0605-ykUOVze.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 
	- none — calves warm from squat + leg press
- **Working:** 
	- 3 × 15 @ **35 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** ONE load across all 3 sets (W7's 37.5→35→30 was invalid); full stretch

## 5. Plank

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0464-CosupLu.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 
	- none
- **Working:**
