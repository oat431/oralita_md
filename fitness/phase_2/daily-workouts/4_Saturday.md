# Saturday — Full Body

Focus: Deadlift + front squat. Time: ~60 min.

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (cycle/walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]`

---

## 1. Barbell Deadlift (conventional)

- **Warm-up ladder:** 40 kg × 5 → 50 kg × 3 → 60 kg × 2 (rest 60–90 sec between ramp sets)
- **Working:** 1 × 5 @ **70 kg** — one work set only
- **Standard:** flat back is non-negotiable; if form breaks, drop to 60 kg and report it

## 2. Barbell Front Squat

- **Warm-up ladder:** 20 kg (bar) × 5 → 25 kg × 3 → 35 kg × 2
- **Working:** 3 × 5 @ **37.5 kg** — rest 2 min
- **Standard:** elbows high; same load across work sets

## 3. Lat Pulldown

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 12.5–15 kg × 10–12 (first back exercise; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **27.5 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** torso still; full controlled pull

## 4. Incline Dumbbell Press

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 12.5–15 kg per hand × 10–12 (first chest work; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **27.5 kg per hand** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** controlled touch and press

## 5. Reverse Lunges

- **Warm-up:** none — legs warm from deadlift + front squat
- **Working:** 3 × 8/leg @ **18 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** clean balance and knee tracking

## 6. Seated Cable Row

- **Warm-up:** none — back warm from pulldown
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **35 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** full range; no jerking
