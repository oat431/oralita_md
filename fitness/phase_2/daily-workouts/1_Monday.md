# Monday — Upper

Focus: Barbell bench press + OHP. Time: ~60 min.

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (cycle/walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]`

---

## 1. Barbell Bench Press

- **Warm-up ladder:** 25 kg × 5 → 32.5 kg × 3 → 42.5 kg × 2 (rest 60–90 sec between ramp sets)
- **Working:** 3 × 5 @ **47.5 kg** — rest 2–3 min
- **Standard:** bar touches chest; no bounce; same load across all working sets

## 2. Barbell OHP

- **Warm-up ladder:** 20 kg (bar) × 5 → 25 kg × 3 → 30 kg × 2
- **Working:** 3 × 5 @ **35 kg** — rest 2 min
- **Standard:** strict press, no leg drive (repeat — W7 was Hard)

## 3. Chest-Supported Row

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 15–17.5 kg × 10–12 (first back exercise; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **32.5 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** full range; same load across work sets

## 4. Face Pulls

- **Warm-up:** none — back is warm from the row
- **Working:** 3 × 12 @ **20 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** pull toward forehead; finish with external rotation

## 5. Tricep Pushdown

- **Warm-up:** none — triceps worked hard in bench + OHP
- **Working:** 2 × 12 @ **20 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** exactly 2 sets; no torso swing
