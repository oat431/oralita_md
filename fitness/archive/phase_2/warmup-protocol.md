# Warm-Up Protocol — Phase 2

Back to: `[[phase2-program-overview]]`

## Why this exists

The warm-up is not wasted time. It raises tissue temperature, lubricates joints, and rehearses the movement pattern before load. **Skipping it means your first working set becomes your warm-up** — the set feels harder, moves slower, and risks sloppier form at the heaviest load of the day.

Time cost: **8–12 minutes per session**. Cheap insurance.

---

## 1. General warm-up — every session (5–8 min)

1. 5 min on the cycle or treadmill (brisk walk)
2. Dynamic movements, no static stretching before lifting:
   - Leg swings — 10/leg
   - Arm circles — 10 forward, 10 backward
   - Hip circles — 10 each direction
   - Bodyweight squats — 10, slow
   - Torso twists or cat-cow — 10
3. If you do the first barbell lift of the day, also do the empty bar practice (below)

---

## 2. Barbell warm-up ladder — before EVERY barbell lift

**Rule:** 3 ramp sets, then the working sets. Never jump straight from the rack to the working weight.

| Step | Load | Reps |
|------|------|------|
| Set 1 | ~50% of working weight | 5 |
| Set 2 | ~70% | 3 |
| Set 3 | ~90% | 2 |
| Then | Working weight | Working sets |

- Rest 60–90 sec between ramp sets, then take the full 2–3 min before the first working set.
- If 50% is lighter than the empty bar (20 kg), just use the empty bar.
- Warm-up sets are **never logged** as working sets.

### Where are the exact ladders?

Each lift's ladder is written **inline under the exercise** in your day notes:

- `[[daily-workouts/1_Monday]]` — bench press, OHP
- `[[daily-workouts/2_Tuesday]]` — back squat
- `[[daily-workouts/4_Saturday]]` — deadlift, front squat

*If a working weight changes: multiply by 0.5 / 0.7 / 0.9 and round to the nearest 2.5 kg.*

---

## 3. Machines and accessories

No full ladder needed.

- **First exercise for a muscle group:** 1 light set of 10–12 reps at ~50–60% of the working load, then rest ~60 sec before the first working set.
- **Later exercises for the same muscle group:** already warm — go straight to the working weight.

Example, Tuesday: Leg Press is the first leg machine → 1 light set. Leg Curl and Calf Raises follow → no extra warm-up sets.

---

## 4. Wednesday — run/walk

- **3–5 min brisk walk** before the first jog interval.
- The walk intervals inside the workout are part of the dose, not the warm-up.
- Total is still **30 minutes** — the warm-up walk counts inside that 30.

---

## 5. Thursday kettlebell (optional)

- 2 min marching in place + 10 unloaded hip hinges before round 1.
- Then the circuit at the prescribed RPE.

---

## Rules

- Warm-up sets are never logged as working sets.
- Sharp pain or a catching joint during a warm-up → stop that lift and report it.
- The warm-up never becomes extra volume: the ramp sets are low-rep and easy by design.
- Feeling rushed is not a reason to skip it — drop a low-priority accessory instead, never the warm-up.

---

*Checklist: did I warm up today? If not, the first working set paid for it — and it will feel heavier than it should.*
