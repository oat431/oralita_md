# Warm-Up Protocol — Phase 3 (Home)

Back to: `[[phase3-program-overview]]`

## Why this exists

Same reason as Phase 2: the warm-up raises tissue temperature, lubricates joints, and rehearses movement patterns. **Skipping it means your first working set becomes your warm-up** — heavier, slower, sloppier. Time cost: 8–12 minutes. Cheap insurance.

---

## 1. General warm-up — every session (5–8 min)

1. **Treadmill** — 5 min brisk walk or light incline walk.
2. Dynamic movements (no static stretching before lifting):
   - Leg swings — 10/leg
   - Arm circles — 10 forward, 10 backward
   - Hip circles — 10 each direction
   - Bodyweight squats — 10, slow
   - Torso twists or cat-cow — 10
   - Push-up machine or wall push-ups — 5–8 easy (Upper days)
3. Then the first exercise's specific warm-up (written inline in the day note).

---

## 2. Kettlebell and dumbbell movements

No full ladder needed — loads are light compared to your barbell numbers.

- **First exercise for a muscle group:** 1 light set of 8–12 reps at ~50–60% of the working load, rest ~60 sec, then the working sets.
- **Later exercises for the same muscle group:** already warm — go straight to the working weight.
- Example, Tuesday: Bulgarian split squat is the first leg exercise → 1 light set of 6/leg. Goblet squat and single-leg RDL follow → no extra warm-up sets.

---

## 3. Push-up progressions

- Before your working push-up sets: 1 easy set of 5–8 on an **easier** progression (e.g., knee push-ups before incline work sets; wall push-ups before knee work sets).
- The working progression itself is decided by the note's Standard line.

---

## 4. Cardio days (Wednesday + Sunday)

- **Wednesday:** 3–5 min brisk walk before the first jog interval. Counts inside the 30 minutes.
- **Sunday:** start the treadmill slow for 3 min (easy pace, no incline), then settle into the target zone. Counts inside the 30–40 minutes.

---

## 5. Thursday kettlebell (optional morning)

- 2 min marching in place + 10 unloaded hip hinges before round 1. Then the circuit at RPE 5/10.

---

## Rules

- Warm-up sets are never logged as working sets.
- Sharp pain or a catching joint during a warm-up → stop that movement and report it.
- The warm-up never becomes extra volume: ramp sets are low-rep and easy by design.
- Feeling rushed is not a reason to skip it — drop the last accessory instead, never the warm-up.

---

*Checklist: did I warm up today? If not, the first working set paid for it — and it will feel heavier than it should.*
