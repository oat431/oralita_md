# Tuesday — Lower (Home)

> ⚠️ **Backup note.** Gym is primary again — use this only when the gym is closed / storm / can't leave. Gym version: `[[daily-workouts/gym/2_Tuesday]]`

Focus: Single-leg strength + squat pattern. Time: ~60 min.

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (treadmill walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]`

---

## 1. Bulgarian Split Squat

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0410-qx4fgX7.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 6/leg, bodyweight only, shallow range (first leg exercise; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8/leg @ **bodyweight** — rest 90 sec between legs
- **Standard:** rear foot elevated on a chair (the GIF shows the split stance — elevate once balance is solid). Slow 2-sec lowering. Week 9 is bodyweight only — add the 10 kg kettlebell in goblet position only when all 3 sets are clean
- **This is the money movement at home:** it loads each leg with most of your 120 kg bodyweight

## 2. Kettlebell Goblet Squat

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0534-ZA8b5hc.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — legs warm from split squats
- **Working:** 3 × 12 @ **10 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** chest tall, elbows inside knees, full depth, no bouncing

## 3. Single-Leg Dumbbell Deadlift (RDL)

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/1757-gKozT8X.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — posterior chain warm from squats
- **Working:** 3 × 10/leg @ **10 kg** (kettlebell held in one hand) — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** flat back, hinge only — shin stays vertical, feel the hamstring stretch. *Dumbbell handles arrived? → 12.5 kg per hand, both hands*

## 4. Standing Calf Raises (single-leg, on a step)

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/1490-6HmFgmx.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — calves warm
- **Working:** 3 × 12–15/leg @ **bodyweight** — rest 45 sec
- **Standard:** full stretch at the bottom, pause at the top. One load across all 3 sets — no mid-set drops (the W7 lesson)

## 5. Plank

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0464-CosupLu.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none
- **Working:** 3 × 40 sec — rest 30 sec
- **Standard:** no sagging; record the actual time. *(GIF shows a twist variant — ignore the twist)*

## 6. Side Planks

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0705-RKjH6Lt.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none
- **Working:** 2 × 30 sec/side — rest 30 sec
- **Standard:** straight line shoulder-to-ankle; no hip drop
