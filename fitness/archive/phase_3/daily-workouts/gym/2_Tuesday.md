# Tuesday — Lower (Gym)

Focus: Back squat + leg accessories. Time: ~55 min. **Re-entry week** — every set Medium or easier.

> Storm / gym closed? → `[[daily-workouts/home/2_Tuesday]]`

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (cycle/walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]` (barbell ladders replace the KB/DB rules)

---

## 1. Barbell Back Squat

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/1436-Gnfo4FM.gif)

- **Warm-up ladder:** rest 60–90 sec between ramp sets
	- 20 kg (bar) × 5
	- 30 kg × 3
	- 35 kg × 2
- **Working:** 3 × 5 @ **40 kg** — rest 2–3 min
- **Standard:** ONE load across all 3 sets. Brace hard; thighs parallel or below. *(Re-entry: 42.5 → 45 in the weeks ahead — only on clean sets.)*

## 2. Leg Press

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0739-10Z2DXU.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 30 kg × 10 (first leg machine; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **60 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** controlled depth; do not lock the knees

## 3. Lying Leg Curl

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0586-17lJ1kr.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — hamstrings warm from squat + leg press
- **Working:** 3 × 10 @ **30 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** two-second lowering phase

## 4. Calf Raises

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0605-ykUOVze.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — calves warm from squat + leg press
- **Working:** 3 × 15 @ **35 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** ONE load across all 3 sets; full stretch

## 5. Plank

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0464-CosupLu.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none
- **Working:** 3 × 35 sec — rest 30 sec *(re-entry: back to 40 sec next week if clean)*
- **Standard:** no sagging; record the actual time. *(GIF shows a twist variant — ignore the twist)*
