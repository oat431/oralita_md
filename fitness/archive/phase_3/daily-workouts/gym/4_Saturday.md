# Saturday — Full Body (Gym)

Focus: Deadlift + front squat. Time: ~60 min. **Re-entry week** — every set Medium or easier.

> Storm / gym closed? → `[[daily-workouts/home/4_Saturday]]`

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (cycle/walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]` (barbell ladders replace the KB/DB rules)

---

## 1. Barbell Deadlift (conventional)

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0032-ila4NZS.gif)

- **Warm-up ladder:** rest 60–90 sec between ramp sets
	- 40 kg × 5
	- 50 kg × 3
	- 60 kg × 2
- **Working:** 1 × 5 @ **65 kg** — one work set only
- **Standard:** flat back is non-negotiable; if form breaks, drop to 60 kg and report it. *(Re-entry: 70 kg was clean-Easy in W8 — it returns next week if this set feels light.)*

## 2. Barbell Front Squat

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0042-zG0zs85.gif)

- **Warm-up ladder:** rest 60–90 sec
	- 20 kg (bar) × 5
	- 25 kg × 3
	- 30 kg × 2
- **Working:** 3 × 5 @ **35 kg** — rest 2 min
- **Standard:** elbows high; same load across work sets

## 3. Lat Pulldown

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/2330-LEprlgG.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 15 kg × 10 (first back exercise; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **27.5 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** torso still; full controlled pull

## 4. Incline Dumbbell Press

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0314-ns0SIbU.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 12.5 kg per hand × 10 (first chest work; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **25 kg per hand** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** controlled touch and press

## 5. Reverse Lunges

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0381-SSsBDwB.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — legs warm from deadlift + front squat
- **Working:** 3 × 8/leg @ **16 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** clean balance and knee tracking

## 6. Seated Cable Row

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0861-fUBheHs.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — back warm from pulldown
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **32.5 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** full range; no jerking
