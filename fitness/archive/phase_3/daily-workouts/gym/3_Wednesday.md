# Wednesday — Run/Walk + Core (Post-Flu Protocol)

Focus: Easy 30-minute movement + core. Time: ~45 min. **This week is walk-first** — your lungs get a vote.

> Storm / gym closed? → `[[daily-workouts/home/3_Wednesday]]` (treadmill at home)

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (cycle/walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]`

---

## 1. Run/Walk — Post-Flu Version

- **Working:** 30 min total. **Start walking.** Add light jog only if: breathing feels 100% AND heart rate stays ≤137. No 4-min jog intervals this week — that protocol returns next week if this session is clean.
- **Log:** minutes + distance + avg HR in `[[logs/daily/run-walk-log]]` — all three numbers, every time
- **Gate:** chest tightness, cough, or unusual shortness of breath → stop, walk it off, report it. Flu-A hit your lungs; the lungs decide the pace this week.
- **Standard:** NO bonus running

## 2. Dead Bugs

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0276-iny3m5y.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — core warm from the walk
- **Working:** 2 × 10/side — rest 30 sec
- **Standard:** slow and controlled, lower back pressed down

## 3. Side Planks

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0705-RKjH6Lt.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none
- **Working:** 2 × 30 sec/side — rest 30 sec
- **Standard:** straight line, no hip drop

## 4. Dumbbell Lateral Raises

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0334-DsgkuIt.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 4–5 kg × 10 (first shoulder work; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 2 × 12 @ **8 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** strict — full ROM, no shrug, no lean, 2-sec lowering. The 8 kg gate stays

## 5. Dumbbell Curls (optional)

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0294-NbVPDMW.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 7.5 kg × 10 (first arm work; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 2 × 10 @ **15 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** no swinging; skip if energy is low — recovery week, not ego week
