# Blood Panel — Baseline (Phase 3)

> One-time clinic draw at Phase 3 start. Reference point for the 12-month visceral-fat (<10) and overall health goals. **Book it in Week 9–10.**

## Pre-draw instructions

- **Fast 8–12 hours** (water allowed). Morning draw, skip breakfast.
- No alcohol 48h before. No hard training the evening before (lipids and liver enzymes distort).
- Ask for a **printed report with reference ranges** — we log values AND their ranges.

## Request this panel (standard + cheap in Thailand)

| Group | Tests | Why at 120 kg / BMI 38 |
| ----- | ----- | ---------------------- |
| Glucose | Fasting glucose | Diabetes screen — the #1 risk at this BMI |
| Glucose | **HbA1c** | 3-month average sugar — more reliable than one fasting reading |
| Lipids | Total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, Triglycerides | Baseline before weight loss improves them |
| Liver | ALT, AST | Fatty liver is common at high visceral fat |
| Kidney | Creatinine, eGFR | High protein diet = worth one baseline |
| Metabolic | **Uric acid** | Cheap in Thai panels; high BMI raises gout risk |
| Optional | Vitamin D, hs-CRP | If the clinic price is fine |

## Retest cadence

- **6 months** after this draw (losing 15+ kg changes every number above). Monthly draws are pointless — HbA1c is a 3-month average and lipids swing ±5–10% biologically, so monthly results are expensive noise.
- **Conditional recheck:** if anything comes back ▲ (e.g., HbA1c ≥5.7, high uric acid), recheck that marker alone at **3 months**.
- Between draws, the free weekly proxies — blood pressure, weight, waist — cover most of the risk surface.
- Bring the old report to the next draw for comparison.

## Glossary & Targets — full names, plain meaning, numbers to maintain

> **Baseline** = whatever your first draw shows (goes in the Results table above). **Target** = the number to move toward and hold. "In lab range" means the clinic's printed reference range wins — use their numbers.

| Alias | Full name | Plain English — what it actually is | Target to maintain |
| ----- | --------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Fasting glucose | Blood sugar (fasting) | Sugar in your blood after 8–12 h without food | <100 mg/dL |
| HbA1c | Hemoglobin A1c (glycated hemoglobin) | Your **3-month average** blood sugar — the most important number at BMI 38 | <5.7% |
| Total cholesterol | Total cholesterol | All blood cholesterol combined | <200 mg/dL |
| LDL | Low-Density Lipoprotein cholesterol | "Bad" cholesterol — the kind that clogs arteries | <100 mg/dL |
| HDL | High-Density Lipoprotein cholesterol | "Good" cholesterol — cleans the arteries | ≥40 mg/dL, ideally ≥60 |
| Triglycerides | Triglycerides | Fat circulating in your blood after meals | <150 mg/dL |
| ALT | Alanine Aminotransferase | Liver enzyme — high = liver under stress (fatty liver shows up here) | In lab range (≈7–56 U/L) |
| AST | Aspartate Aminotransferase | Second liver enzyme — same signal as ALT | In lab range (≈10–40 U/L) |
| Creatinine | Creatinine | Muscle waste product — tests how well kidneys filter | In lab range (0.7–1.3 mg/dL) |
| eGFR | estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate | Kidney function as a percentage — higher = better | ≥90 mL/min/1.73m² |
| Uric acid | Uric acid | Waste from protein breakdown — high = gout and kidney-stone risk | <7.0 mg/dL |
| Vitamin D *(optional)* | 25-hydroxyvitamin D | Bone, immune system, mood | 30–50 ng/mL |
| hs-CRP *(optional)* | high-sensitivity C-Reactive Protein | Whole-body inflammation level | <1 mg/L |

**Units:** mg/dL = milligrams per deciliter · U/L = units per liter · ng/mL = nanograms per milliliter.

**Why this matters:** weight loss moves every row. Expect glucose, HbA1c, LDL, triglycerides, uric acid — and blood pressure — to all drop with each 5 kg lost. The 6-month redraw measures exactly that.
