# Upper Body

Focus: barbell bench + OHP. Time: ~60 min. **W1 re-entry** — every set Medium or easier. Hard = data, not a problem: repeat that load next week.

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (walk/cycle + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]` (barbell ladders)

---

## 1. Barbell Bench Press

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0025-EIeI8Vf.gif)

- **Warm-up ladder:** rest 60–90 sec between ramp sets
	- 20 kg × 5
	- 25 kg × 3
	- 30 kg × 2
- **Working:** 3 × 5 @ **32.5 kg** — rest 2–3 min
- **Standard:** bar touches chest, no bounce, same load across all working sets

## 2. Barbell OHP

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/1456-wdRZISl.gif)

- **Warm-up ladder:** rest 60–90 sec
	- 20 kg (bar) × 5
	- 22.5 kg × 3
- **Working:** 3 × 5 @ **25 kg** — rest 2 min
- **Standard:** strict, no leg drive

## 3. Chest-Supported Row

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0606-aaXr7ld.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 12.5 kg × 10 (first back exercise; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **22.5 kg** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** full range; same load across work sets

## 4. Face Pulls

- **Warm-up:** none — back is warm from the row
- **Working:** 3 × 12 @ **15 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** pull toward forehead; finish with external rotation

## 5. Tricep Pushdown

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0241-gAwDzB3.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — triceps worked hard in bench + OHP
- **Working:** 2 × 12 @ **15 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** exactly 2 sets; no torso swing
