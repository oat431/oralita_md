# Walk Cardio

Focus: brisk walking — the fat-loss cardio. Sessions: Tue/Thu (40–50 min) + Sat (long route). **No running in W1.**

**Before anything:** 3–5 min easy walking to start; counts inside the session.

---

## The Session

- **Tue / Thu:** 40–50 min brisk walk — condo-area loop. Pace: can talk in full sentences but feel warm (talk test). Avg HR usually 105–130.
- **Sat:** condo → WE Fitness Esplanade route, **5.2 km (~55–65 min)**. MRT back. Optional: 10 min stretching at the gym after.
- **Log every session** in `[[logs/daily/walk-log]]`: minutes + distance + avg HR — all three, every time.
- **HR cap 130** — above = slow down.
- **Standard:** no bonus distance in W1. Feet and joints at 119 kg adapt first.

## Core Finisher (Sat only, optional)

- Dead Bugs — 2 × 10/side, rest 30 sec
- Side Planks — 2 × 30 sec/side, rest 30 sec
- Plank — 2 × 30 sec, rest 30 sec

## Rules

- **W1: exactly 3 walks (Tue/Thu/Sat).** Daily walking starts W2 only with zero foot complaints — add one day at a time, cap 60 min.
- Foot/shin pain persisting >48 h → report; walks drop to 2/wk.
- **Shoes:** if your pair is >1 year old or the soles are worn flat, replace before W2 — the #1 walk-injury cause at high bodyweight.
- The walk is cardio, not a commute: on strength days, take the MRT.
