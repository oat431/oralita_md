# Warm-Up Protocol — Gym

Back to: `[[program-overview]]`

## Why this exists

The warm-up raises tissue temperature, lubricates joints, and rehearses movement patterns. **Skipping it means your first working set becomes your warm-up** — heavier, slower, sloppier. Time cost: 8–12 minutes. Cheap insurance.

---

## 1. General warm-up — every gym session (5–8 min)

1. **5 min** brisk walk or light cycle.
2. Dynamic movements (no static stretching before lifting):
   - Leg swings — 10/leg
   - Arm circles — 10 forward, 10 backward
   - Hip circles — 10 each direction
   - Bodyweight squats — 10, slow
   - Torso twists or cat-cow — 10
3. **Activation** (2–4 min, RPE 5–6) — the phase most lifters skip (demos: `../knowledge/fundamentals.md` §6–7):
   - Glute bridges — 2 × 10 (before squat/DL days)
   - Band pull-aparts — 2 × 15 (before pressing days)
   - Dead bugs — 2 × 6/side (bracing)
4. Then the first exercise's specific warm-up ladder (written inline in the exercise note).

---

## 2. Barbell ladders

- **First exercise for a movement pattern:** ramp sets from the note (e.g. 20 × 5, 25 × 3, 30 × 2), rest 60–90 sec between.
- **Later exercises for the same muscle group:** already warm — go straight to the working weight.

---

## 3. Walk days (Tue / Thu / Sat)

- 3–5 min easy pace to start; counts inside the session. No special warm-up needed for walking + core.

---

## Rules

- Warm-up sets are **never logged** as working sets.
- Sharp pain or a catching joint during a warm-up → stop that movement and report it.
- The warm-up never becomes extra volume: ramp sets are low-rep and easy by design.
- Feeling rushed is not a reason to skip it — drop the last accessory instead, never the warm-up.

---

*Checklist: did I warm up today? If not, the first working set paid for it — and it will feel heavier than it should.*
