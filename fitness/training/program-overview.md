## Cardio (walking)

- Tue/Thu: 40–50 min brisk walk. Talk test; avg HR usually 105–130.
- Sat: condo→Esplanade route, 5.2 km (~55–65 min), MRT back. Optional 10-min stretch at the gym after.
- **Log all three numbers every walk** (minutes, distance, avg HR) → `logs/daily/walk-log.md`.
- **HR cap 130** — above → slow down. **No jogging in W1** (joints at 119 kg).
- Feet are the limiter: worn-out shoes (>1 yr old) = the #1 walk-injury cause at high bodyweight. Foot/shin pain persisting >48 h → report, drop to 2 walks.
