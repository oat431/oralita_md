# Wednesday — Run/Walk + Core (Home)

Focus: 30-minute intervals + core. Time: ~45 min. Treadmill or outdoor — your call.

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (treadmill walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]`

---

## 1. Run/Walk Intervals

- **Warm-up:** 3–5 min brisk walk first (counts inside the 30 minutes)
- **Working:** 30 min total — **4 min jog / 2 min walk × 5**
- **Treadmill:** distance from the console. **Don't hold the handles** — grip kills the watch's HR reading. Outdoor: start the Running workout on the watch.
- **Log:** minutes + distance + avg HR in `[[logs/daily/run-walk-log]]` — all three numbers, every time
- **Zone check:** avg HR above 137 → slow the jogs. Below 117 → that's fine, honest data
- **Standard:** NO bonus running

## 2. Dead Bugs

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0276-iny3m5y.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — core warm from running
- **Working:** 2 × 10/side — rest 30 sec
- **Standard:** slow and controlled, lower back pressed into the mat

## 3. Side Planks

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0705-RKjH6Lt.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none
- **Working:** 2 × 30 sec/side — rest 30 sec
- **Standard:** straight line, no hip drop

## 4. Dumbbell Lateral Raises

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0334-DsgkuIt.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 2 kg × 10 slow (first shoulder work; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 2 × 15 @ **2 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** strict — full ROM, no shrug, no torso lean, 2-sec lowering. 2 kg looks light; done strict and slow it burns. This is a form exercise, not a loading exercise

## 5. Dumbbell Curls (optional)

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0294-NbVPDMW.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 5 kg × 10 (first arm work — the run does not warm the arms; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 2 × 10 @ **7.5–10 kg per hand** (dumbbell handles) — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** no swinging; skip if time or recovery is limited. *No handles yet → skip; the KB one-arm row covers biceps this week*
