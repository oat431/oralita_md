# Monday — Upper (Home)

Focus: Push + press + rows. Time: ~60 min.

**Before anything:** general warm-up 5–8 min (treadmill walk + dynamic moves) → `[[warmup-protocol]]`

---

## 1. Push-Ups (machine or incline)

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0493-B1EVP9F.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 easy set of 5–8 on an easier progression (wall → knee → incline)
- **Working:** 3 × 8–12 — progression: wall → incline → knee → full. Use the push-up machine if it makes the progression cleaner
- **Standard:** full ROM, body straight, no sagging hips. Same progression for all 3 sets

## 2. Dumbbell Floor Press

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/1298-rg59QCH.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 5–7.5 kg × 10 (first weighted press; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **12.5 kg per hand** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** slow 2-sec lowering, elbows stop at the floor, press to lockout. *No dumbbell handles yet? → Kettlebell floor press, one arm at a time: 3 × 10/side @ 10 kg*

## 3. Dumbbell Overhead Press

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/1456-wdRZISl.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 5 kg × 10 (rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 8 @ **10 kg per hand** — rest 90 sec
- **Standard:** strict, no leg drive, no lean-back. *(GIF shows barbell — dumbbell pattern is the same)*

## 4. Kettlebell One-Arm Row

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0541-g9AsZ8P.gif)

- **Warm-up:** 1 light set — 5–7.5 kg × 10 (first back exercise; rest ~60 sec after)
- **Working:** 3 × 10/side @ **10 kg** — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** flat back, pull to the hip, 2-sec lowering. Brace the free hand on a chair

## 5. Band Face Pulls

- **Warm-up:** none — back is warm from the row
- **Working:** 3 × 12–15 @ **band** (door anchor at face height) — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** pull toward the forehead, finish with thumbs back (external rotation). Band loop or tube both fine

## 6. Diamond Push-Ups

![form|150](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset/main/videos/0283-soIB2rj.gif)

- **Warm-up:** none — chest/triceps are warm
- **Working:** 2 × 8–12 (on knees if needed) — rest 60 sec
- **Standard:** exactly 2 sets; hands form a diamond under the chest; elbows track back
