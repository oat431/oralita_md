# Food Log — Thai Freestyle Tracking System (Phase 3 — LIVE)

> The kcal audit for the 2,050/day target. Built for street food: **no app, no full macro logging** — fixed anchors + Thai dish table + one weekly audit day. The check-in Kcal column (✓ / ▲ / ⚠) is live from Week 9.

## Why not a calorie app?

App logging breaks in Thailand: stalls have no labels, the same dish from two vendors varies ±30% (oil, sugar, portion), and the Thai food databases are unreliable. Logging every meal = logging guesses. This system logs **structure** daily and audits with real numbers once a week.

## The System — three parts

### 1. Fixed anchors (count once, forget forever)

- **Protein anchor:** every meal starts with a palm-size lean protein. Target 160 g/day = 640 kcal. Amounts in `[[meal-templates-160g-protein]]` (protein rows still valid; ignore the old kcal totals — they're from the 2,400 era).
- **Rice anchor:** rice is the kcal dial in Thailand. **1 ladle (thap pi) ≈ 130 g cooked ≈ 170 kcal. Budget: 3 ladles/day.**
- **Fat anchor:** ~60 g/day of *visible* fat — cooking oil, egg yolks, curry cream. This is what makes the same dish swing ±300 kcal between stalls.

**7-Eleven rule:** packaged food has a real label — the kcal is printed on it. When in doubt, 7-Eleven beats the stall. The label beats this table.

**About the estimates (±20%):** a "650 kcal" dish is really 520–780. Over a week of mixed dishes, the error averages out — the table is usable as-is. No calibration homework required. The weekly audit below is the tiebreaker if the scale ever stalls.

### 3. Weekly audit — 1 day/week (Thursday)

- Count **everything** that day using the dish table + a kitchen scale for one rice portion (learn what a ladle really weighs at your usual source).
- Total it at night. Day lands 2,000–2,200 → anchors are calibrated, trust them the rest of the week.
- Frequency: every Thursday for the first 3 weeks → then once a month. Thursday is a rest day — perfect.

## The day structure (2,050 kcal target)

| Slot | What | ~Kcal |
| ---- | ---- | ----- |
| Breakfast — fixed | 3 boiled eggs + 1 scoop whey + fruit | ~500 |
| Lunch — freestyle | Any dish-table item with a protein anchor | ~650 |
| Dinner — freestyle | Any dish-table item with a protein anchor | ~650 |
| Snack | Moo ping ×2 **or** Greek yogurt 200 g | ~250 |
| **Total** | | **~2,050** |

- **Free at any size:** clear soups, vegetables, chili / vinegar / lime. Sugary nam jim (sweet chili) is NOT free.
- The 3-ladle rice budget lives inside the lunch and dinner slots.

## The boredom question — answered

Same-meal-forever is **not** required for the cut to work. What's required is kcal staying in the band without daily math. Two fixed anchors + two free slots pulling from ~17 dishes = 30+ combinations. Bodybuilders eat repetitive *structures*, not identical meals — and they're paid to suffer. You're paid in results: keep the variety, keep the anchors.

## Scoring (the Kcal column in `logs/daily/training-log-phase3.md` check-ins)

- **✓** = day assembled within structure (~2,000–2,200)
- **▲** = over (fried meal, dessert, liquid calories, extra rice)
- **⚠** = under (missed a protein anchor)
- **6/7 ✓ per week** = the target. ▲ days are fine if planned (80/20 — see `[[foods-to-avoid]]`).

## Watch-outs (details in `[[foods-to-avoid]]`)

1. Liquid calories are the #1 budget killer — Thai iced tea/coffee = 250–350 kcal each.
2. Deep-frying doubles or triples the kcal of the same meat.
3. Coconut-milk curries are weekend food, not daily food.
4. The 80/20 rule: 80% structured, 20% life. One bad meal doesn't ruin the week.
