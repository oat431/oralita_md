# Run/Walk Log — Phase 3

> The marathon (12–18 month goal) runs on this file. One row per cardio session — **Wednesday AND Sunday**. Fill immediately after the session while the watch still has the workout on screen.

## Frequency (important)

- **Wednesday:** 30 min intervals — 4 min jog / 2 min walk × 5.
- **Sunday:** 30–40 min easy Zone 2 (HR 117–137).
- Daily walking is separate — it goes in the steps column of the check-in table, not here.
- No bonus running. At 120 kg, dose control beats enthusiasm.

## How to get the numbers (Xiaomi Watch S4)

1. Start a **Running** workout (outdoor) or the **Treadmill** mode before the session.
2. After: workout history → this session → read **duration, distance, avg pace, avg HR**.
3. Treadmill: distance from the console is fine; **don't hold the handles** — grip kills the wrist HR reading.
4. If the watch shows time-in-zone, grab Zone 2 as well.

## Targets

| Session | Minutes | Avg HR | Standard |
| ------- | ------- | ------ | -------- |
| Wed intervals | 30 exactly | 117–137 (Zone 2) | 4 min jog / 2 min walk × 5 |
| Sun easy | 30–40 | 117–137 | walk / light incline; easy = easy |

**Above 137 avg → slow down.** Below 117 → honest data, still a valid session.

## Why this file exists

Phase 2: minutes logged 1 of 4 weeks. Zero progression decisions were possible. Phase 3 rules: every cardio session produces three numbers — minutes, distance, avg HR. No numbers, no review credit.

*Progression (W10+) comes only from this table, not from feelings.*
