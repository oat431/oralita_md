# Blood Pressure Log — Template

> Home cuff monitoring. The missing cardiovascular metric at 121 kg — resting HR alone doesn't cover it.

## Measurement protocol (every time)

1. Seated, 5 min quiet rest first. Back supported, feet flat, no talking.
2. Arm at heart level, cuff on bare skin.
3. Empty bladder. No coffee, exercise, or smoking in the 30 min before.
4. **Two readings, 1 min apart — record BOTH + the average.** Single readings lie.
5. Morning measurements preferred (before food/meds).

⚠️ **Cuff check:** valid if it's an **upper-arm** cuff. Wrist cuffs under-read and are not worth logging — if yours is a wrist model, say so and we'll use the gym/clinic machine instead.

## Classification (adult, from clinic guidelines)

| Category | Systolic | Diastolic |
| -------- | -------- | --------- |
| Optimal | <120 | <80 |
| Elevated | 120–129 | <80 |
| Stage 1 | 130–139 | 80–89 |
| Stage 2 | ≥140 | ≥90 |

**Target at your bodyweight:** get under 130/80, trending toward <120/80 as weight drops.
**Action rule:** two separate sessions averaging ≥140/90 → book a doctor visit, don't wait for Sunday review.

BP drops ~1 mmHg per 1 kg lost — this log and the scale log tell the same story.
