# oralita_md

My personal Obsidian vault. Not the technical brain (that lives in [swe-knowledge](https://github.com/oat431/swe-knowledge) and [general-knowledge](https://github.com/oat431/general-knowledge)), just my actual life written down: fitness, my home lab, musicals, paper rabbit holes at midnight, wishes for friends' gacha pulls. It syncs between my laptop and tablet, and it's public mostly so I can't get lazy with it.

## What's in here

- **[fitness/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/fitness)**: training programs and gym knowledge (protein meal templates, warmup protocols, what to do when the gym is packed), plus the boring-but-useful stuff like blood panel baselines and monthly measurements.
- **[home-lab/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/home-lab)**: my server rabbit hole. Installation guides, deployments, database and microservice components, and Obsidian LiveSync so this vault itself stays in step across devices.
- **[daily-paper/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/daily-paper)**: paper reading notes. Mostly AI and CS: Bitcoin, DeepSeek, MiMo, Qwen, spec-driven development, whatever made me stop scrolling.
- **[personal/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/personal)**: the grab bag. AI landscape notes for 2026, my brand and visual identity, meta-learning, and musicals (Hamilton, Epic, Les Mis, La La Land, Anastasia, and counting).
- **[audio/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/audio)**: headphone nerdery. IEM listening setup, EQ experiments, gear verdicts.
- **[wishes/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/wishes)**: a library of short wish snippets, from luck to new beginnings. It started as a Viking Rune Wish that accidentally pulled a friend's rare gacha item, and now it's a whole system. See the [overview](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/blob/main/wishes/00_overview.md) for the origin story.
- **[writing/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/writing)**: writing craft knowledge and prompts. The practice folders stay on my machines only.
- **[templates/](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/tree/main/templates)**: fill-in masters for writing, fitness, research, finance, brand, and online cards.
- **media-art/**: photos and visual experiments. Still an empty shelf (git ignores empty folders), photos will land here eventually.

## What's not in here

Some folders are gitignored on purpose: writing practice, drafts, and a couple of personal notes. Public repo, private life. Anything with real PII never leaves my machines.

## How it runs

- **[CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/blob/main/CLAUDE.md)** is the operating charter for AI agents working in this vault. Yes, I gave my AI partner house rules. It works better that way.
- Plain Obsidian conventions: wikilinks, YAML frontmatter, numbered notes, one overview note per folder where it matters.
- Everything here is public domain under [The Unlicense](https://github.com/oat431/oralita_md/blob/main/LICENSE). Take what's useful.
