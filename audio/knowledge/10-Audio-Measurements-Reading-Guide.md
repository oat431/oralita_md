# Audio Measurements Reading Guide

Measurements are filters for bad engineering and exaggerated claims. They are not a complete measurement of enjoyment.

## Frequency-response graph

Shows relative output level by frequency. Use it to discuss tonal balance. Always check:

- measurement rig and compensation;
- smoothing;
- insertion depth;
- left/right channel matching;
- whether the graph is raw or compensated.

Do not compare two graphs from different rigs as if they were identical rulers.

## THD / distortion

Total harmonic distortion shows unwanted harmonic energy relative to the fundamental. Very high distortion can be audible; low distortion below audibility is not a reason to prefer one device over another.

## SINAD

SINAD combines signal, noise, and distortion into one ratio. It is useful for identifying seriously poor electronics, but once performance is transparent for the use case, a higher SINAD number may have no audible benefit.

## Noise floor and dynamic range

These describe how quiet the electronics are relative to the maximum useful signal. Sensitive IEMs make noise more important than a large power number.

## Output impedance

Check this when pairing a source with low-impedance or multi-driver IEMs. A high value can interact with the IEM's impedance curve and change frequency response.

## Channel balance and crosstalk

- **Channel balance:** left and right output levels match, especially at low listening volume.
- **Crosstalk:** unwanted leakage between channels. Lower is better, but very good values are normally inaudible.

## Interpreting measurements responsibly

| Result | Reasonable conclusion |
|---|---|
| Large FR peak/dip | Likely tonal change |
| High distortion at normal level | Investigate audible distortion |
| Excellent SINAD | Electronics are likely transparent, not necessarily enjoyable |
| High output power | Useful only for a load that needs it |
| High sample-rate support | Compatibility feature, not proof of better sound |
| One impressive graph | Ask for the complete measurement set |

## Trusted-use mindset

Use measurements to rule out noise, distortion, channel problems, and poor compatibility. Then use fit, music, controlled listening, and personal preference to choose.

## Personal lab application

The MX1's high output capability is not by itself an upgrade for the D1. Because the D1 is efficient, check noise, gain, output impedance, and volume safety—not merely the largest mW figure.
