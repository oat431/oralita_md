# 4.4 mm Balanced Audio

## Connector versus circuit

A 4.4 mm Pentaconn plug is only a connector format. It does not prove that a signal is balanced. The device's output circuit determines whether it is truly balanced.

A real balanced headphone output normally has separate positive and negative amplifier legs for the left and right channels. This can provide higher voltage swing and better channel separation, depending on the design.

## Balanced does not mean automatically better

A balanced output can be useful when it provides:

- enough extra power for a difficult headphone;
- a lower noise floor in a particular setup;
- convenient cable compatibility;
- improved channel separation that is actually measurable.

It does not automatically make an efficient IEM more detailed, wider, or more musical.

## Your cable rules

Correct for the COZOY D1:

```text
BGVP MX1 4.4 mm output
→ direct 4.4 mm-to-IEM cable
→ COZOY D1
```

For the Sony WH-1000XM5, use the MX1's 3.5 mm single-ended output and the Sony-supplied 3.5 mm stereo cable. The XM5 has a single 3.5 mm input; this is not a balanced headphone input.

Specialized balanced-to-single-ended conversion products exist, but they are not a normal passive adapter category, provide no useful benefit here, and must be explicitly designed for the source. Do not assume that a generic cable or adapter is safe merely because its plugs fit. When the cable's wiring is unclear, use the MX1's 3.5 mm single-ended output instead.

Avoid:

```text
3.5 mm single-ended output
→ passive 3.5-to-4.4 adapter
→ device's 4.4 mm balanced output
```

A passive adapter changes the plug format; it does not convert a single-ended signal into a balanced circuit. More importantly, some balanced outputs must not be connected to a common ground through an inappropriate adapter.

## Polarity and wiring

A balanced cable must have the correct left/right and polarity wiring for the device. A cable terminated for one proprietary pinout can be unsafe or simply incompatible with another product. Use normal TRRRS 4.4 mm wiring from a reputable maker and do not experiment with unknown adapters.

## Why the MX1 is enough

The MX1's 4.4 mm output has abundant power for the efficient COZOY D1. Use low gain and the direct cable. The reason to use 4.4 mm here is the MX1's available output/cable compatibility—not a guarantee of a magical tonal upgrade.
