# IEM and Headphone Specifications

## Impedance (Ω)

Impedance is the load presented to the amplifier. It is not a quality score and it is not constant across all frequencies. A low-impedance IEM may be easy to make loud but can be more sensitive to source output impedance.

## Sensitivity

Sensitivity describes how loud a transducer becomes from a defined input. It may be specified per volt (`dB/V`) or per milliwatt (`dB/mW`). Always check the unit.

- High sensitivity + low impedance: usually easy to drive, but may reveal hiss and need careful volume control.
- Low sensitivity: may need more voltage/current and amplifier headroom.

The COZOY D1's published specification is commonly listed as 32 Ω and 100 dB/Vrms; RAA reports a measured average impedance near 33.2 Ω and sensitivity near 113.15 dB/mW. Measurement conventions explain why the figures look different; do not mix them directly.

## Driver type

| Driver | Basic idea | What it does not guarantee |
|---|---|---|
| DD / dynamic | Diaphragm moved by a voice coil and magnet | Automatically better bass or realism |
| BA / balanced armature | Small armature driver, often used for mids/treble | Automatically better detail |
| Planar | Large thin diaphragm driven across its surface | Automatically better technical performance |
| Hybrid | Multiple driver types | Better tuning without good crossover design |

Driver count and exotic diaphragm materials are not reliable quality scores. Judge the measured response, distortion, fit, and your preference.

## Frequency response

Frequency response is the relative output level across frequency. It is the closest measurement to the tonal balance you hear, but measurements depend on the coupler, insertion depth, tips, and compensation target.

Common regions:

| Region | Approximate role |
|---|---|
| Sub-bass | Deep rumble and impact |
| Mid-bass | Warmth, punch, body |
| Lower mids | Chords, male vocals, fullness |
| Upper mids / presence | Vocal clarity and shout/intelligibility |
| Treble | Cymbal energy, brightness, air |

## What specifications are useful?

Useful: impedance curve, sensitivity with units, frequency-response measurement, distortion, channel matching, fit information, and warranty.

Weak marketing signals: 5 Hz–50 kHz frequency-range claims, driver-count claims without measurements, “graphene” or “aviation-grade” labels, and “Hi-Res” logos by themselves.

## Applied to D1 + MX1

The D1 is efficient enough that the MX1's available power is not the bottleneck. If you want a different experience, first investigate the D1's tonal balance, eartip seal, and EQ.
