# Audition (The Fools Who Dream)
> from La La Land (2016): performed by Mia (Emma Stone) during her audition

---

## Learning Objectives
By studying this song, you will be able to:
- Tell a **story in Past Simple** ("She leapt," "She tumbled," "She lived")
- Understand **metaphor** ("sky with no ceiling," "the sunset inside a frame")
- Use **infinitive of purpose** ("To give us new colors to see")
- Recognize **parallel structure** ("Here's to the ones who dream... Here's to the hearts that ache...")

## Grammar Points

### 1. "Used to" — เคย (แต่ไม่ทำแล้ว)
**ปรากฏในเพลง:** ท่อน 1-2

| Expression | Example | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **used to + verb** | "My aunt used to live in Paris" | ป้าเคยอยู่ปารีส (แต่ไม่อยู่แล้ว) |
| **used to + verb** | "She used to come home" | เธอเคยกลับบ้าน (แต่ไม่กลับแล้ว) |

> ⚠️ **Thai Speaker Trap:** "used to" ≠ "be used to" ≠ "get used to"
> - "I **used to** smoke." = เคยสูบ (แต่เลิกแล้ว)
> - "I **am used to** smoking." = คุ้นเคยกับการสูบ
> - "I **am getting used to** smoking." = กำลังเริ่มคุ้นเคย

### 2. Parallel Structure: โครงสร้างคู่ขนาน
**ปรากฏในเพลง:** ท่อน 11-14, 26-28

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Here's to...** | "Here's to the ones who dream" |
| **Here's to...** | "Here's to the hearts that ache" |
| **Here's to...** | "Here's to the mess we make" |

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **The...** | "The painters" |
| **The...** | "and poets" |
| **The...** | "and plays" |

> 💡 **Why this matters:** Parallel structure สร้างจังหวะและเน้น — ใช้ใน speech, song, และ essay

🔗 ดูเพิ่ม: [24 Parallel Structure](https://github.com/oat431/general-knowledge/tree/main/English%20Skill/07%20Writing%20%26%20Style/24%20Parallel%20Structure.md)

---

### 3. Infinitive of Purpose: "to" + verb (เพื่อ)
**ปรากฏในเพลง:** ท่อน 23

- "To give us new colors to see" = เพื่อให้เราเห็นสีสันใหม่ๆ

| Structure | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| **to + verb** (purpose) | "I came here **to learn**." |
| **to + verb** (purpose) | "She jumped **to feel** alive." |
