# ❤️ 02 — Love Wishes

> Companion to [[01-Luck-Wishes]]. Every snippet is **source-verified** unless marked `[craft]` (original, written for this library), and **code blocks contain ONLY the copy-paste wish** — transliteration, translation, and sources sit below each block.

**Occasion tags:** 💘 confession/crush · 💌 singles hoping · 💍 anniversary/couples · ❤️ everyday love

## Quick-Pick Map

| Occasion | First pick | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| 💘 Confession | Sappho fr. 16 — "whatever one loves is most beautiful" | reframes the risk as beauty; fearless without being romantic mush |
| 💌 Singles | Thai ขอให้เจอเนื้อคู่ไวๆนะ, or Ṛgveda 10.85.42 | the Thai destined-partner wish is the culture's go-to; the Vedic hymn is the oldest wedding blessing we have |
| 💍 Anniversary | Ṛgveda 10.85.42, Thai ขอให้รักกันตลอดไป | "Be ye not parted… grow old in your own abode" — 3,000 years of service |
| ❤️ Everyday | Latin *si vis amari, ama* | Senecan, pocket-sized, impossible to argue with |

⚠️ **Honesty first — the "love runes" myth:** Sigrdrífumál does **NOT** contain *ástrúnar* (love-runes) — verified against the full Old Norse text (the rune list is Sigrúnar, Ölrúnar, Bjargrúnar, Brimrúnar, Limrúnar, Málrúnar, Hugrúnar — victory, ale, help, surf, limb, speech, mind). Never cite "Eddic love runes." What *is* attested: the Freyja line below, and runes with real meanings (gift, joy, horse) whose **love readings are modern**.

## 1. ᚠ Viking Runes (Old Norse)

### The luck runes' love cousins (attested meanings only)

| Rune | Name | Attested meaning | Modern love reading? |
|------|------|------------------|---------------------|
| ᚷ | **Gebo** | gift (OE *Gyfu*: "generosity brings credit and honour") | modern: partnership — *label it* |
| ᚹ | **Wunjo** | joy (OE: "bliss he enjoys who knows not suffering") | modern: harmony — *label it* |
| ᛖ | **Ehwaz** | horse (OE: "a joy to princes") | modern: partnership/"twinned" — *label it* |

⚠️ All three are Elder Futhark / Anglo-Saxon futhorc; the Viking Age used Younger Futhark. The love associations are modern esoteric. Use the flavor, keep the labels honest.

### What's actually attested

**Freyja's own line** — Gylfaginning 24: *"Songs of love are well-pleasing to her; it is good to call on her for furtherance in love."* This is the authenticated Viking love invocation. (Freyr, her brother, is for *árs ok friðar* — plenty and peace, not love.)

### Snippets

**1. The Freyja call** ❤️💘 `[craft — attested invocation used as blessing]`

```
Henni líkaði vel mansöngr. Á hana er gott at heita til ásta.
```

*EN:* "Songs of love are well-pleasing to her; it is good to call on her for furtherance in love." *(ON pronunciation notes: ð = th as in "bathe"; á ≈ "ah")*
*Source:* Gylfaginning 24, Prose Edda (Snorri). The one authenticated Norse love line — open or close a love wish with it.

**2. Hávamál 93 — the no-fault-in-loving rule** ❤️💌

```
Ástar firna skyli engi maðr
annan aldregi.
```

*EN:* "Fault for loving let no man find ever with any other." (Bellows) *(ON: ð in maðr = th as in "bathe"; á ≈ "ah")*
*Source:* Hávamál 93, Poetic Edda. Use: for a friend in love with the "wrong" person by someone else's reckoning — or as a warm blessing on any honest love.

**3. The rune-string love wish** 💘💍 `[craft — runes attested, love readings modern]`

```
ᚷ ᚹ ᛖ — Gebo, Wunjo, Ehwaz: gift, joy, the twinned road.
May love find you, and stay awhile.
```

*Note:* rune meanings "gift/joy/horse" are attested; the pairing-with-love reading is modern. Labeled, delivered, still charming.

**4. Hávamál 92 — the longing line** 💘 *(for the confessing side)*

```
sá er vill fljóðs ást fá
```

*EN:* "…he who longs for a maiden's love" *(ON: fljóðs — ð = th as in "bathe")* — a fragment, not a full blessing. Use: only as a *quote* atop a confession pep-talk (`[craft]` wrapper needed).

## 2. 🦅 Latin

**1. Omnia vincit Amor: et nos cedamus Amori** ❤️💍

```
Omnia vincit Amor: et nos cedamus Amori.
```

*EN:* "Love conquers all; let us too yield to Love."
*Source:* Vergil, *Eclogues* 10.69. The Roman line for every love that refuses to be sensible.

**2. Si vis amari, ama** 💌💘❤️

```
Si vis amari, ama.
```

*EN:* "If you wish to be loved, love."
*Source:* Seneca, *Epistulae Morales* 9.6 (quoting Hecaton: "...monstrabo amatorium sine medicamento"). The pocket philosophy of love — send to anyone, any occasion.

**3. Vivamus, mea Lesbia, atque amemus** 💍❤️

```
Vivamus, mea Lesbia, atque amemus
```

*EN:* "Let us live, my Lesbia, and love."
*Source:* Catullus 5.1. ⚠️ Romantic partners only — it's an erotic invitation poem (swap "mea Lesbia" for their name at your own risk; the name-carrying line is the ship of Theseus of pet names).

**4. Amor et melle et felle est fecundissimus** 💍 *(the honest one)*

```
Amor et melle et felle est fecundissimus.
```

*EN:* "Love is most fruitful in honey and in gall."
*Source:* Plautus, *Cistellaria* 69. Use: for long-married friends — the ones who'll laugh, because they know.

## 3. 🏛️ Ancient Greek

**1. Sappho fr. 1 — the Aphrodite line** 💘🙏 *(hymn-prayer — respect register)*

```
Ποικιλόθρον' ἀθανάτ' Ἀφρόδιτα
```

*Translit:* Poikilothron' athanat' Aphrodita · *EN:* "Deathless Aphrodite of the many-hued throne." (Vandiver: "Iridescent-throned Aphrodite, deathless")
*Source:* Sappho fr. 1.1 (ed. Smyth 1900; preserved via Dionysius of Halicarnassus). An opening of a real hymn — lend solemnity, not a meme.

**2. Sappho fr. 16 — whatever one loves** 💘💌❤️

```
ἔμμεναι κάλλιστον, ἔγω δὲ κῆν' ὄττω
τις ἔραται
```

*Translit:* emmenai kalliston, egō de kēn' ottō tis eratai · *EN:* "…is most beautiful; but I say it is whatever one loves." (ed. Wharton 1895)
*Source:* Sappho fr. 16.3–4. The perfect confession-adjacent line: what you love is what's beautiful — no permission needed.

**3. Aristotle — the friend as another self** ❤️💌

```
ἕτερος γὰρ αὐτὸς ὁ φίλος ἐστίν
```

*Translit:* heteros gar autos ho philos estin · *EN:* "For the friend is another self." (Rackham)
*Source:* Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* 1170b6–7 (ed. Bywater). ⚠️ Cite 1170b — **not** the widely-copied "1166a31" (verified wrong).

**4. The wedding cry** 💍🍾

```
Ὑμὴν ὦ Ὑμέναιε
```

*Translit:* Hymēn ō Hymenaie · *EN:* "Hymen, O Hymenaios!" — the ancient wedding shout.
*Full line:* `Ὑμὴν, ὦ Ὑμέναιε, γάμῳ ἐπὶ τῷδε χαρείης` — "Hymen, O Hymenaios, may you rejoice in this wedding!" (Theocritus, *Idyll* 18 closing refrain; also Euripides, *Troades* 310 ff.)
*Source:* Theocritus Id. 18; the cry also closes Sappho's own epithalamia.

**The four loves (reference table — LSJ-verified):**

| Word | Sense in Ancient Greek |
|------|------------------------|
| ἔρως *erōs* | "love, mostly of the sexual passion" |
| φιλία *philia* | "affectionate regard, friendship, usu. betw. equals" |
| στοργή *storgē* | "love, affection — esp. of parents and children" |
| ἀγάπη *agapē* | noun essentially **post-classical** (LXX/NT: "brotherly love, charity", 1 Cor 13.1); the verb ἀγαπάω is older |

*Craft blessing built on it:* "ἔρως · φιλία · στοργή · ἀγάπη — may all four find you, and none leave." `[craft]`

## 4. 🕉️ Sanskrit

**1. Ṛgveda 10.191.2 — go together** 💍💌

```
सं गच्छध्वं सं वदध्वं सं वो मनांसि जानताम् ।
```

*Translit:* saṃ gacchadhvaṃ saṃ vadadhvaṃ saṃ vo manāṃsi jānatām ·
*EN:* "Assemble, speak together: let your minds be all of one accord." (Griffith)
*Source:* Ṛgveda 10.191.2 — the **final hymn of the Rigveda**; recited as a unity blessing (also at the close of the wedding hymn's tradition). The most famous "two minds, one accord" verse in Sanskrit.

**2. Ṛgveda 10.85.42 — the bridal hymn** 💍

```
इहैव स्तं मा वि यौष्टं विश्वमायुर्व्यश्नुतम् ।
क्रीळन्तौ पुत्रैर्नप्तृभिर्मोदमानौ स्वे गृहे ॥
```

*Translit:* ihaiva staṃ mā vi yauṣṭaṃ viśvam āyur vyaśnutam | krīḷantau putrair naptṛbhir modamānau sve gṛhe
*EN:* "Be ye not parted; dwell ye here; reach the full time of human life. With sons and grandsons sport and play, rejoicing in your own abode." (Griffith)
*Source:* Ṛgveda 10.85.42 — the Vedic wedding hymn (Sūryā's Bridal). Our oldest verified wedding blessing — perfect anniversary wish.

**3. Bhagavad Gītā 12.13 — friendly and compassionate** ❤️

```
अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।
निर्ममो निरहंकारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥
```

*Translit:* adveṣṭā sarva-bhūtānāṃ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca | nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ samaduḥkhasukhaḥ kṣamī
*EN:* "…who hates no being, who is friendly and compassionate, free from egoism…" (Telang)
*Source:* Bhagavad Gītā 12.13 — opening of the ideal devotee's portrait. The "love as character" blessing.

**4. The Pali mettā wish** 💌❤️🙏 *(shared with future peace wishes; prayer register)*

```
sabbe sattā bhavantu sukhitattā
```

*EN:* "May all beings be happy!" · companion line: `sukhino vā khemino hontu` — "May they be happy and safe!"
*Source:* Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta, Sutta Nipāta 1.8 — the classic Theravāda loving-kindness wish (chanted in Thai as สพฺเพ สตฺตา ภวนฺตุ สุขิตตฺตา). Metta is love without wanting anything back — the rare form of the four.

**Vocabulary (reference):** प्रेम *prema* "love, tender regard" · स्नेह *sneha* "affection for; friendship" (root: "cling" — affection that sticks) · काम *kāma* "desire, longing; love" (also the god of Desire, Kāma; first stir of creation, RV 10.129.4).

## 5. 🇹🇭 Thai

**1. For the confession** 💘 *(warm/friendly register)*

```
ขอให้สมหวังในความรักนะ
```

*Translit:* kɔ̌ɔ hâi sǒm-wǎng nai khwaam rák ná · *EN:* "May your hopes in love come true."
The classic send-off before someone confesses. (นะ soft — for elders, นะคะ/ครับ.)

**2. For the single friend** 💌 *(the culture's go-to)*

```
ขอให้เจอเนื้อคู่ไวๆนะ
```

*Translit:* kɔ̌ɔ hâi jəə nʉ́a-khûu wai-wai ná · *EN:* "May you meet your destined one soon."
เนื้อคู่ = the fated partner — someone you've likely shared a past life with (cf. บุพเพสันนิวาส, Pali *pubbe-sannivāsa*: "lived together in a former life"). ⚠️ Note: despite the similar vibe, เนื้อคู่'s etymology is native Thai (เนื้อ+คู่) — do **not** claim it comes from Chinese 缘 (unverified).

**3. For the couple — anniversary** 💍 *(the default)*

```
ขอให้รักกันตลอดไป
```

*Translit:* kɔ̌ɔ hâi rák kan tà-lɔ̀ɔt pai · *EN:* "May you love each other forever."
The standard Thai anniversary/wedding blessing. Softer alternatives:

```
อยู่ด้วยกันจนแก่เฒ่า
```

*Translit:* yùu dûai kan con kàe thâo · *EN:* "May you grow old together." (warm, folksy)
And the slightly more formal: `ขอให้รักกันยั่งยืน` — "may your love endure."

**4. Courting season** 💘 *(very informal — friends only)*

```
จีบติดนะ
```

*Translit:* jìip tìt ná · *EN:* "Hope your courting succeeds."
Playful; "ขอให้จีบติด" also common. Safer classic: `ขอให้สมหวังนะ`. Never use with elders.

**5. For someone starting over** 💌 *(warm)*

```
ขอให้มีคนดีๆ เข้ามาในชีวิตนะ
```

*Translit:* kɔ̌ɔ hâi mii khon dii-dii khâo maa nai chii-wít ná · *EN:* "May good people come into your life."

**6. The light one** ❤️ *(slightly cheeky — peers only)*

```
โชคดีในความรักนะ
```

*Translit:* chôhk dii nai khwaam rák ná · *EN:* "Good luck in love." — the tongue-in-cheek cousin of โชคดีนะ.

*Culture note: Thai love-vocabulary to know —* เนื้อคู่ *fated partner* · คู่แท้ *true match* · คู่บุญ *blessed match* · คู่เวร *karmic match* (the one you owe) · พรหมลิขิต *fate written by Brahma*.

## The Viking Love Wish — Reproducible Recipe 💘

Mirroring the luck recipe, with maximal honesty:

1. **Pick 3 runes:** ᚷ Gebo (gift), ᚹ Wunjo (joy), ᛖ Ehwaz (the twinned road) — *meanings attested; love readings modern, keep the labels*
2. **Rune string:** `ᚷᚹᛖ`
3. **Incantation line** naming the runes
4. **Seal with Freyja** — the *attested* invocation: "it is good to call on her for furtherance in love" (Gylfaginning 24)
5. Send before the confession / on the anniversary / just because.

Assembled example `[craft]`:

```
ᚷ ᚹ ᛖ
By Gebo's gift, Wunjo's joy, Ehwaz's twinned road —
may love find you, and stay awhile.
Freyja, hear this one.
```

## ⚠️ Misuse Notes

- **Never cite "ástrúnar" as Eddic love runes — they don't exist** (verified against the full ON text)
- **Love readings of ᚷ/ᚹ/ᛖ are modern** — the file labels them; you do too
- **Sappho fr. 1, the mettā line, Gītā 12.13 are prayers** — solemnity register, not jokes
- **agapē is post-classical** (LXX/NT) as a noun — don't present "four Greek loves" as one classical system, it's a later curation
- **Catullus 5 is for romantic partners only**
- **Heartbreak / condolence: not here** — grief gets [[04-Wishing-Note-Guide]] rules; comfort wishes come with [[07-Peace-Wishes]] (planned)
- **Crossover pick:** romantic gambles (crush DMs, confessions at long odds) → Ovid's *Audentem Forsque Venusque iuvat* lives in [[01-Luck-Wishes]]
