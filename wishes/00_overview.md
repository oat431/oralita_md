# Best Wishes Overview

> **Origin story:** A friend asked for best wishes for her gacha game. I sent a **Viking Rune Wish** — and it accidentally pulled her the rare item. This folder is where that accident becomes a system: a library of short, crafted **wish snippets** for every kind of hoping.
>
> Craft-side companion (how to write wishes for real people on real occasions): [[04-Wishing-Note-Guide]] · Fill-in masters: `templates/writing/wishing.md`
> **This folder is different:** occasion notes are *social* writing; wish snippets are *ritual* writing — short, symbolic, half-magical. They borrow from charms, runes, blessings, and gacha superstition.

## The Two Kinds of Wishing

| | Social wish | Ritual snippet |
|---|---|---|
| Example | "Happy birthday, Mint!" | A rune-carved luck charm for a gacha pull |
| Measured by | recipient feels *seen* | recipient feels *armed* — hope with a shape |
| Length | 1–4 sentences | 1–3 lines, often poetic/archaic |
| Register | matches relationship | matches *tradition* (Norse, Thai, Japanese, generic mystical) |

## Taxonomy: Types of Wishes

Cross-checked against how charm traditions actually categorize blessings (Chinese numismatic charms, Thai Sak Yant, feng shui symbols, Japanese ema/omikuji — all converge on roughly the same set):

### The "chance" subtype — gacha wishes

Luck wishes deserve a sub-branch of their own for this project: **gacha superstition is real, documented player folklore** — lucky chairs, lucky postures (standing for the final pull), timing rituals, shrine visits before pulling, chat-channel incantations. A crafted rune/charm snippet fits perfectly into that ritual ecosystem. That's exactly why the Viking Rune Wish "worked": it gave her pull *ceremony*, and ceremony is what makes hope feel active.

## Wish Delivery Mechanisms (folklore inventory)

From Wikipedia's *Wish* and charm traditions — each is a potential **format** for a snippet:

- **Blow & burn:** birthday candles, dandelion seeds
- **Toss:** coins into wishing wells/fountains, rice, lentils at midnight
- **Write & hang:** ema plaques (Japan), red ribbons on trees (China), sky lanterns
- **Draw & cast:** omikuji fortune slips, rune casting (the Viking wish's family)
- **Wear & carry:** charms, amulets, Sak Yant tattoos, red string bracelets
- **Speak & seal:** blessings, novenas, toasts, incantations — *words themselves as the charm* ← this is what a snippet library does

## Craft rules for snippets (inherited + extended)

1. **Specific beats grand** — even in ritual register ("may the golden light touch your tenth pull" > "may you have good luck")
2. **Tradition = flavor, respect = rule** — borrow symbols, don't parody living religions; Sak Yant and novenas carry real weight for real people
3. **Ground backward, wish forward** — still applies: name their actual hope before blessing it
4. **A snippet is a gift, not an essay** — 3 lines max for chat delivery
5. **Never promise outcomes** — "may the runes favor you," not "this will get you the 5-star"
